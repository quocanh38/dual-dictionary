The full guides are written in Vietnamese by the app developer (Senior Business Development, TMA) — from real experience learning and teaching IELTS/TOEIC while working full-time. This page summarizes the key ideas in English for hackathon judges; links go to the Vietnamese originals.

New: the offline pipeline (31 Oct 2026)

  • From a work reading to an offline pack — how a handover email becomes a ~10KB lesson: Meta drafts, editor reviews, install once, learn forever offline.
  • 30-day plan with 33 offline packs — 1 pack/day (10–20 min), weeks 1–2 workplace B1, weeks 3–4 academic + IELTS lab, review every day.
  • Night shifts, weak signal, still learning TOEIC — 15-minute one-time offline setup checklist + 3 sample night-shift sessions.

Study method (11 posts)

A 7-part series on learning English without translating in your head (Reading, Listening, Writing frames, Speaking reflexes, 6 Vietnamese-learner mistakes, 4-week plan) — plus FSRS/spaced-repetition explainers matching the algorithm actually running in the app, and an offline-dictionary setup guide.

TOEIC (9 posts)

Learning on a phone with no signal, dead-minute sessions, Part 5–6 vocabulary, Part 7 speed reading, business English from real emails — written for office workers, not students.

IELTS (12 posts + new)

30 minutes a day, band descriptors in plain language, Task 2 writing frames, Part 2 cue cards, collocations by topic, IPA pronunciation — all aimed at busy adults.

New: Meta you can see (Nov 2026)

Photo-to-reading: photograph a printed notice, Meta vision (muse-spark-1.3, HTTP 200 verified) transcribes 333 characters into a draft that walks the same review road. Pack covers: 3 Muse Image illustrations (display-only, packs untouched). Full evidence: Why Meta page.

The 33 lesson packs prove what Meta models can generate; these guides prove the builder understands the learner. Same voice, same audience (working adults on flaky networks), same offline-first philosophy. Total: 34 posts, ~48,000 words, all linked into the app's Help and Practice views.
