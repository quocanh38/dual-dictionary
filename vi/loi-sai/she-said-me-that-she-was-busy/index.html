Lỗi sai thường gặp

Đừng nói “She said me that she was busy”

“say” không đi với tân ngữ chỉ người (không nói “say me”); khi nêu người nghe phải dùng “tell + tân ngữ + (that)”. Muốn giữ “say” thì bỏ tân ngữ người nghe: “She said (that) she was busy”.

Câu đúng

  • ✓ She told me that she was busy.
  • ✓ The manager told us the meeting had been cancelled.
  • ✓ Please tell the receptionist that I am waiting.
