Lỗi sai thường gặp

Đừng nói “She mentioned about the delay”

“mention” là ngoại động từ, đi thẳng với tân ngữ (“mention something”), không cần “about”. Chỉ dùng “about” sau danh từ “mention”: “There was no mention about the delay”.

Câu đúng

  • ✓ She mentioned the flight delay in her email.
  • ✓ He mentioned that the deadline had been extended.
  • ✓ Nobody made any mention of the missing report.
