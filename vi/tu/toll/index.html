Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • pay a toll; toll road; toll of war

Dễ nhầm

  • a toll is a fee for using a road or bridge, or a serious loss caused by an event; toll can also mean sound a bell

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “Traffic was backed up at the toll booth.” (Xe cộ ùn lại ở trạm thu phí.)
