Nghĩa tiếng Việt của

political prisoner

Cụm từnoun phraseBiên soạn riêng

1. A person imprisoned primarily for political reasons, such as opposing a government

Cấu trúc: release political prisoners · be held as a political prisoner · political-prisoner status

The organization called for the release of political prisoners.

Tổ chức kêu gọi thả các tù nhân chính trị.

The government denied that the detainees were political prisoners.

Chính phủ phủ nhận những người bị giam là tù nhân chính trị.

Lưu ý: A person imprisoned primarily for political reasons, such as opposing a government; classification can be disputed and should be attributed where contested.
