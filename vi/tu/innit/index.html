Điểm cần nhớ

  • “nice, innit?”; innit → nonstandard “isn't it?”

Dễ nhầm

  • innit is a nonstandard, informal British form of isn't it, often used as a conversational tag; use isn't it in formal writing

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • ““Cold today, innit?” he said.” (“Hôm nay lạnh nhỉ?” anh nói.)
