Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • widely supported; supported by evidence; be supported by someone

Dễ nhầm

  • supported is the past form of support and means helped, backed or held up; supported by evidence means evidence provides justification

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “The proposal was supported by most members.” (Đề xuất được đa số thành viên ủng hộ.)
