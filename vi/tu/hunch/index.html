Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • have a hunch; hunch your shoulders; hunch → feeling without proof/lean forward

Dễ nhầm

  • a hunch is an idea or feeling without proof; as a verb, hunch means bend the upper body forward

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “I had a hunch that the keys were in the car.” (Tôi linh cảm chìa khóa ở trong xe.)
