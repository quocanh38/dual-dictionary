Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • shrink a sweater; shrink in fear; shrink from responsibility

Dễ nhầm

  • shrink means become smaller or make something smaller; the past tense is shrank or shrunk, and the participle is shrunk

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “The wool sweater shrank in the wash.” (Áo len bị co lại khi giặt.)
