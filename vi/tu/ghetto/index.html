Điểm cần nhớ

  • historical ghetto; ghettoisation; ghetto → segregated urban area

Dễ nhầm

  • ghetto historically refers to an area where a minority group was forced to live; modern use can be sensitive, so avoid using it casually for any poor neighbourhood

Mẫu câu giá trị
