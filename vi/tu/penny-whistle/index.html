penny whistle

Cụm từphraseBiên soạn riêng

1. A small simple fipple flute, traditionally made of metal or wood

Cấu trúc: play a penny whistle · a tune on the penny whistle · penny-whistle music

He played a folk tune on a penny whistle.

Anh thổi một giai điệu dân gian bằng sáo penny whistle.

The band included a penny whistle and fiddle.

Ban nhạc có sáo penny whistle và đàn fiddle.

Lưu ý: A small simple fipple flute, traditionally made of metal or wood; also called a tin whistle or Irish whistle.
