horse mackerel

Định nghĩa tiếng Anh (WordNet 3.1)

noun

  1. a California food fish (Synonyms: jack mackerel, Spanish mackerel, saurel, Trachurus symmetricus)
  2. large elongated compressed food fish of the Atlantic waters of Europe (Synonyms: saurel, Trachurus trachurus)
  3. largest tuna; to 1500 pounds; of mostly temperate seas: feed in polar regions but breed in tropics (Synonyms: bluefin, bluefin tuna, Thunnus thynnus)
