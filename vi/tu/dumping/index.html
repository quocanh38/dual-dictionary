Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • illegal dumping; price dumping; dumping → leaving or selling in excess

Dễ nhầm

  • dumping is leaving waste somewhere improperly, or selling goods abroad at unusually low prices; the context determines which sense

  • “The factory was fined for dumping chemicals in the river.” (Nhà máy bị phạt vì đổ hóa chất xuống sông.)
