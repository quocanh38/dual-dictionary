Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • forcing someone to leave; forcing a decision; force → make happen

Dễ nhầm

  • forcing is the -ing form of force and means making someone act or causing an outcome, sometimes against their wishes

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “They were accused of forcing workers to stay late.” (Họ bị cáo buộc ép công nhân ở lại muộn.)
