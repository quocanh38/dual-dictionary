Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • school detention; immigration detention; detention → being kept somewhere

Dễ nhầm

  • detention is the state of being kept in a place and not allowed to leave; at school it can mean a punishment requiring a student to stay after class

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “He stayed after school in detention.” (Cậu ấy ở lại sau giờ học để chịu phạt.)
