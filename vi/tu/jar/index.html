Điểm cần nhớ

Dễ nhầm

  • a jar is a container, usually glass, with a wide opening; as a verb, jar can mean make a harsh sound or upset someone

  • “She opened a jar of honey for breakfast.” (Cô mở một hũ mật ong cho bữa sáng.)
