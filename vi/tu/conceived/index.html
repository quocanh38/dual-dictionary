Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • conceived an idea; conceived a child; conceived of something

Dễ nhầm

  • conceived is the past form of conceive, meaning form an idea or become pregnant; conceive of means imagine

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “The plan was conceived during a team meeting.” (Kế hoạch được hình thành trong một cuộc họp nhóm.)
