Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • grind coffee; grind to a halt; grind → crush into small pieces

Dễ nhầm

  • to grind means crush something into smaller pieces by rubbing; grind to a halt means stop gradually and with difficulty

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “She ground the coffee beans before breakfast.” (Cô xay hạt cà phê trước bữa sáng.)
