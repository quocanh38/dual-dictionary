Định nghĩa tiếng Anh (WordNet 3.1)

noun

  1. an ornamented evergreen used as a Christmas decoration
  2. medium to tall fir of western North America having a conic crown and branches in tiers; leaves smell of orange when crushed (Synonyms: amabilis fir, white fir, Pacific silver fir, red silver fir, Abies amabilis)
  3. tall timber tree of central and southern Europe having a regular crown and grey bark (Synonyms: European silver fir, Abies alba)
  4. a terrestrial evergreen shrub or small tree of western Australia having brilliant yellow-orange flowers; parasitic on roots of grasses (Synonyms: flame tree, fire tree, Nuytsia floribunda)
  5. Australian tree or shrub with red flowers; often used in Christmas decoration (Synonyms: Christmas bush, Ceratopetalum gummiferum)

Source: WordNet 3.1 — Princeton University (open license)
