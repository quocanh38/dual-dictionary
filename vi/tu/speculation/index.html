Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • speculation about the future; widespread speculation; pure speculation

Dễ nhầm

  • speculation is an opinion or guess made without all the facts; financial speculation is a separate sense

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “There was widespread speculation about the cause of the delay.” (Có nhiều suy đoán về nguyên nhân sự chậm trễ.)
