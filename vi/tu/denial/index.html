Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • denial of access; deny a charge; denial → refusal to accept/state

Dễ nhầm

  • a denial is a statement that something is not true or a refusal to grant something; denial of access means access was refused

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “The company issued a denial of the allegations.” (Công ty ra thông cáo phủ nhận các cáo buộc.)
