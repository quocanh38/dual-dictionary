noun

  1. an island comprising England and Scotland and Wales (Synonyms: GB)
  2. a monarchy in northwestern Europe occupying most of the British Isles; divided into England and Scotland and Wales and Northern Ireland; `Great Britain' is often used loosely to refer to the United Kingdom (Synonyms: United Kingdom, UK, U.K., Britain, United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland)
