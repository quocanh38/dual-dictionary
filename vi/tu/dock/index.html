Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • at the dock; dock a ship; dock → harbour platform/reduce pay

Dễ nhầm

  • a dock is part of a port where ships load, unload or are repaired; as a verb, dock can mean bring a ship in or reduce pay or points

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “The ship unloaded its cargo at the dock.” (Con tàu dỡ hàng tại bến cảng.)
