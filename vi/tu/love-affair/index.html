love affair

Cụm từnoun phraseBiên soạn riêng

Mục trong từ điển mở: “love-affair”.

1. A romantic or sexual relationship, often secret or outside marriage

Cấu trúc: have a love affair with · a secret love affair · a love affair with music

The novel tells the story of a secret love affair.

Cuốn tiểu thuyết kể câu chuyện về một mối tình bí mật.

The chef has had a lifelong love affair with Italian food.

Đầu bếp say mê ẩm thực Ý suốt đời.

Lưu ý: A romantic or sexual relationship, often secret or outside marriage; figuratively, a strong enthusiasm for something.
