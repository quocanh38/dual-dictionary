Định nghĩa tiếng Anh (WordNet 3.1)

noun

  1. a North American tree of the genus Liquidambar having prickly spherical fruit clusters and fragrant sap (Synonyms: sweet gum tree, bilsted, red gum, American sweet gum, Liquidambar styraciflua)
  2. aromatic exudate from the sweet gum tree (Synonyms: liquidambar)
  3. reddish-brown wood and lumber from heartwood of the sweet gum tree used to make furniture (Synonyms: satin walnut, hazelwood, red gum)

Source: WordNet 3.1 — Princeton University (open license)
