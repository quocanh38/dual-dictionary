Nghĩa tiếng Việt của

make out

Cụm từphrasal verbBiên soạn riêngPhổ biến

1. nhìn, nghe hoặc đọc được điều gì không rõ ràng

Cấu trúc: make out a shape/word · make something out in the distance · can/cannot make out

I could just make out the lighthouse through the fog.

Tôi chỉ vừa nhận ra ngọn hải đăng qua màn sương.

She could not make out the writing on the faded label.

Cô không đọc được chữ trên nhãn đã phai.

2. hiểu hoặc nhận ra ý nghĩa của điều gì

Cấu trúc: make out what somebody said · make something out · make out that...

I cannot make out why the figures do not match.

Tôi không hiểu vì sao các con số không khớp.

He made out that he had never seen the document.

Anh ta nói như thể mình chưa từng thấy tài liệu đó.

3. điền hoặc viết một tài liệu như hóa đơn, séc hay biểu mẫu

Cấu trúc: make out a cheque/bill · make a cheque out to somebody · make out a form

Please make the cheque out to the registered company.

Vui lòng viết séc cho công ty đã đăng ký.

The supplier made out an invoice for the replacement parts.

Nhà cung cấp lập hóa đơn cho các linh kiện thay thế.

Lưu ý: Make out có nhiều nghĩa; riêng make out that somebody is... thường hàm ý người đó trình bày hoặc giả vờ như vậy. Cấu trúc séc có thể là make a cheque out to somebody.

Định nghĩa tiếng Anh (WordNet 3.1)

verb

  1. comprehend
    "I cannot make out what this politician is saying"
  2. try to establish
    "She made out that she know nothing about the crime"
  3. imply or suggest
    "Your remarks make me out to be stupid"
  4. write all the required information onto a form (Synonyms: complete, fill out, fill in)
    "fill out this questionnaire, please!"
    "make out a form"
  5. make out and issue (Synonyms: write out, issue, cut)
    "write out a check"
    "cut a ticket"
  6. kiss, embrace, or fondle with sexual passion (Synonyms: neck)
    "The couple were necking in the back seat of the car"
  7. have sexual intercourse with (Synonyms: sleep together, roll in the hay, love, make love, sleep with, get laid)
    "This student sleeps with everyone in her dorm"
    "Adam knew Eve"
  8. detect with the senses (Synonyms: spot, recognize, recognise, distinguish, discern, pick out)
    "The fleeing convicts were picked out of the darkness by the watchful prison guards"
    "I can't make out the faces in this photograph"
  9. succeed in doing, achieving, or producing (something) with the limited or inadequate means available (Synonyms: cope, get by, make do, contend, grapple, deal)
    "We got by on just a gallon of gas"
    "They made do on half a loaf of bread every day"
  10. proceed or get along (Synonyms: do, fare, come, get along)
    "How is she doing in her new job?"
    "How are you making out in graduate school?"
