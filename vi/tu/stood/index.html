Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • stood in line; stood by a friend; stand → stood

Dễ nhầm

  • stood is the past tense and past participle of stand; stayed means remained in a place or state

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “He stood near the door during the meeting.” (Anh đứng gần cửa trong cuộc họp.)
