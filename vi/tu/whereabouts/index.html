Điểm cần nhớ

  • ask about someone's whereabouts; current whereabouts; whereabouts unknown

Dễ nhầm

  • whereabouts means the place where someone or something is; it is often used when the location is unknown

  • “The police asked about his whereabouts that night.” (Cảnh sát hỏi anh đã ở đâu vào đêm đó.)
