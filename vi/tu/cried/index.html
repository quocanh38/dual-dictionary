Điểm cần nhớ

  • cry for help; cried with joy; cry → cried

Dễ nhầm

  • cried is the past form of cry, meaning shed tears or shout; weep is more formal for shedding tears

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “The child cried when she lost her toy.” (Đứa trẻ khóc khi làm mất món đồ chơi.)
