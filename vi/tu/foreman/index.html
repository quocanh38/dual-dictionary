Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • site foreman; foreman of a jury; foreman → person in charge of workers

Dễ nhầm

  • a foreman supervises workers or leads a jury; the term is gendered, so supervisor or site manager may fit better for any gender
