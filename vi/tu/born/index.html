Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • be born in/on; born to; born and raised; newborn

Dễ nhầm

  • born is usually used with be to describe birth; bear is the verb in formal descriptions of giving birth

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “He was born in Hue and raised in Canada.” (Anh sinh ở Huế và lớn lên ở Canada.)
