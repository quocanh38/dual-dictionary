Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • affordable housing; housing shortage; housing → homes for people

Dễ nhầm

  • housing means houses or flats available for people to live in and is usually uncountable; housing costs can include rent and utilities

Mẫu câu giá trị
