Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • drift away; drift into sleep; drift → move slowly without direction

Dễ nhầm

  • to drift is to move slowly without clear control or direction; a drift can also be a pile formed by wind or water

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “The boat began to drift towards the shore.” (Con thuyền bắt đầu trôi dạt về phía bờ.)
