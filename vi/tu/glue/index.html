Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • glue the pieces together; glue someone to a screen; glue → sticky substance

Dễ nhầm

  • glue is a sticky substance used to join things; as a verb, glue something together means attach it, while glued to a screen is figurative

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “Use glue to attach the label to the box.” (Dùng keo dán nhãn vào hộp.)
