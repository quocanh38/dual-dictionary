come up

Cụm từphrasal verbBiên soạn riêngPhổ biến

Mục trong từ điển mở: “come-up”.

1. xuất hiện hoặc được nhắc đến để mọi người xem xét

Cấu trúc: a problem/question comes up · come up in a meeting · come up with an idea

A technical issue came up during the demonstration.

Một sự cố kỹ thuật phát sinh trong buổi trình diễn.

The subject came up at yesterday's meeting.

Chủ đề đó được nhắc đến trong cuộc họp hôm qua.

2. tiến đến gần hoặc đến lúc xảy ra

Cấu trúc: come up to somebody · an event/date comes up

A stranger came up to me and asked for directions.

Một người lạ tiến đến hỏi đường tôi.

The application deadline is coming up next week.

Hạn nộp đơn sắp đến vào tuần tới.

Lưu ý: Come up with something nghĩa là nghĩ ra/đề xuất, còn a problem comes up nghĩa là vấn đề phát sinh. Khi nói thời điểm sắp đến, thường dùng thì tiếp diễn.

Định nghĩa tiếng Anh (WordNet 3.1)

verb

  1. result or issue (Synonyms: arise)
    "A slight unpleasantness arose from this discussion"
  2. gather or bring together (Synonyms: muster, rally, summon, muster up)
    "muster the courage to do something"
    "she rallied her intellect"
  3. gather (money or other resources) together over time (Synonyms: scrape, scrape up, scratch)
    "She had scraped together enough money for college"
    "they scratched a meager living"
  4. start running, functioning, or operating (Synonyms: go on, come on)
    "the lights went on"
    "the computer came up"
  5. bring forth, usually something desirable
    "The committee came up with some interesting recommendations"
  6. move toward, travel toward something or somebody or approach something or somebody (Synonyms: come)
    "He came singing down the road"
    "Come with me to the Casbah"
  7. move upward (Synonyms: rise, lift, arise, move up, go up, uprise)
    "The fog lifted"
    "The smoke arose from the forest fire"
  8. come up, of celestial bodies (Synonyms: rise, uprise, ascend)
    "The sun also rises"
    "The sun uprising sees the dusk night fled..."
  9. come to the surface (Synonyms: surface, rise up, rise)
  10. get something or somebody for a specific purpose (Synonyms: line up, get hold, find)
    "I found this gadget that will serve as a bottle opener"
    "I got hold of these tools to fix our plumbing"
  11. originate or come into being (Synonyms: arise, bob up)
    "a question arose"
  12. be mentioned
    "These names came up in the discussion"

Source: WordNet 3.1 — Princeton University (open license)
