life sentence

Cụm từnoun phraseBiên soạn riêng

Mục trong từ điển mở: “life-sentence”.

1. A prison sentence for the remainder of a person’s life, though parole eligibility and legal meaning differ by jurisdiction

Cấu trúc: receive a life sentence · serve a life sentence · a life sentence without parole

The judge imposed a life sentence for the crime.

Thẩm phán tuyên án tù chung thân cho tội danh đó.

The law allows parole review for some life sentences.
