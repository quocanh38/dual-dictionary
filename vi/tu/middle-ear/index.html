middle ear

Cụm từphraseBiên soạn riêng

1. The air-filled part of the ear between the eardrum and inner ear, containing the ossicles that transmit sound

Cấu trúc: middle-ear infection · fluid in the middle ear · middle-ear pressure

The infection caused fluid to build up in the middle ear.

Nhiễm trùng khiến dịch tích tụ trong tai giữa.

The middle ear transfers vibrations toward the inner ear.

Tai giữa truyền rung động đến tai trong.
