Định nghĩa tiếng Anh (WordNet 3.1)

goldbrick

noun

  1. anything that is supposed to be valuable but turns out to be worthless
  2. a brick-shaped block that looks like gold but is not
  3. an idle worthless person (Synonyms: goof-off, ne'er-do-well, good-for-nothing, no-account, good-for-naught)
  4. a soldier who performs his duties without proper care or effort

verb

  1. avoid (one's assigned duties) (Synonyms: fiddle, shirk, shrink from)
    "The derelict soldier shirked his duties"
  2. deprive of by deceit (Synonyms: victimize, swindle, rook, nobble, diddle, bunco)
    "He swindled me out of my inheritance"
    "She defrauded the customers who trusted her";"
