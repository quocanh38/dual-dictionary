Nghĩa tiếng Việt của

get out

Cụm từphrasal verbBiên soạn riêngPhổ biến

1. rời khỏi một nơi hoặc thoát khỏi tình huống

Cấu trúc: get out of a car/building · get out safely · get out of doing something

Everyone got out of the building through the rear exit.

Mọi người ra khỏi tòa nhà qua lối sau.

He tried to get out of attending the briefing.

Anh tìm cách tránh tham dự buổi phổ biến thông tin.

2. được tiết lộ hoặc được đưa ra công chúng

Cấu trúc: the news gets out · get a message/story out

The news got out before the company made an announcement.

Tin tức bị lộ trước khi công ty đưa ra thông báo.

They worked quickly to get the safety guidance out to customers.

Họ nhanh chóng gửi hướng dẫn an toàn đến khách hàng.

Lưu ý: Get out of a place là rời khỏi; get out of doing something là tránh làm; when news gets out nghĩa là tin bị lộ. Get something out có thể tách tân ngữ.

Định nghĩa tiếng Anh (WordNet 3.1)

verb

  1. escape potentially unpleasant consequences; get away with a forbidden action (Synonyms: get off, get away, get by, escape)
    "She gets away with murder!"
    "I couldn't get out from under these responsibilities"
  2. be released or become known; of news (Synonyms: break, get around)
    "News of her death broke in the morning"
  3. express with difficulty
    "I managed to get out a few words"
  4. take out of a container or enclosed space (Synonyms: bring out)
    "Get out your best dress--we are going to a party!"
  5. bring, take, or pull out of a container or from under a cover (Synonyms: draw, pull, pull out, take out)
    "draw a weapon"
    "pull out a gun"
  6. move out or away (Synonyms: pull out)
    "The troops pulled out after the cease-fire"
  7. move out of or depart from (Synonyms: exit, go out, leave)
    "leave the room"
    "the fugitive has left the country"

Source: WordNet 3.1 — Princeton University (open license)
