Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • elbow joint; elbow someone aside; elbow → arm joint

Dễ nhầm

  • the elbow is the joint between the upper and lower arm; as a verb, elbow can mean push someone with the elbow or force a way through

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “He rested his elbow on the table.” (Anh chống khuỷu tay lên bàn.)
