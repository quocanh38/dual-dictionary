Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • plead guilty; plead for mercy; plead with somebody

Dễ nhầm

  • plead means make an urgent request or state an answer in court; past forms are pleaded or pled, with pleaded more common in British English

Mẫu câu giá trị
