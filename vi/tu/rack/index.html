Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • coat rack; rack your brains; rack up points

Dễ nhầm

  • a rack is a frame for holding things; rack your brains means think very hard, while rack up means accumulate

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “He hung his coat on the rack.” (Anh treo áo khoác lên giá.)
