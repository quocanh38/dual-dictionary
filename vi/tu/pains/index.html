Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • take pains to do something; growing pains; pains of childbirth

Dễ nhầm

  • pains is plural of pain; in take pains, it means make a careful effort, while in pains it can mean physical suffering

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “She took great pains to explain the process.” (Cô đã rất cố gắng giải thích quy trình.)
