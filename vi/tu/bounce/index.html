Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • bounce a ball; bounce back; bounce a cheque

Dễ nhầm

  • bounce means spring back after hitting a surface or recover from difficulty; rebound is a more formal synonym

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “The ball bounced over the fence.” (Quả bóng nảy qua hàng rào.)
