Điểm cần nhớ

  • proposed changes; proposed to resign; proposed a solution

Dễ nhầm

  • proposed is the past form or participle of propose; propose doing something is common when suggesting an action

  • “They proposed building a new bridge.” (Họ đề xuất xây một cây cầu mới.)
