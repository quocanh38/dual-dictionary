Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • haunt a house; memory haunts someone; haunt → visit repeatedly or trouble

Dễ nhầm

  • to haunt can mean a ghost repeatedly visits a place or a memory keeps troubling someone; a haunt is also a place someone visits often
