Ghi chú sử dụng

Điểm cần nhớ

  • eat → ate → eaten; ate breakfast; ate too much

Dễ nhầm

  • ate is the simple past; eaten is the past participle used with have/has/had

Mẫu câu giá trị

  • “We ate outside because the weather was pleasant.” (Chúng tôi ăn bên ngoài vì thời tiết dễ chịu.)
