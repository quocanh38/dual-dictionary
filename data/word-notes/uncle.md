### Điểm cần nhớ
- an uncle; uncle on your mother’s/father’s side; Uncle Sam
### Dễ nhầm
- uncle is a parent’s brother or an aunt’s husband; English does not mark the relationship by maternal/paternal side in the word itself
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “My uncle on my mother’s side lives near the coast.” (Cậu của tôi sống gần bờ biển.)
