### Điểm cần nhớ
- such a cutie; cutie pie; cutie → attractive or sweet person
### Dễ nhầm
- cutie is an informal affectionate word for someone attractive or endearing; it can sound familiar, so avoid it in formal settings
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Everyone thought the puppy was a cutie.” (Ai cũng thấy chú chó con thật đáng yêu.)
