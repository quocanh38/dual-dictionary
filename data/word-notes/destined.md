### Điểm cần nhớ
- destined to succeed; destined for trouble; destined → expected future
### Dễ nhầm
- destined is usually followed by to plus a verb or for plus a noun and suggests a future that seems decided or likely
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The young player seemed destined for success.” (Cầu thủ trẻ dường như sẽ thành công.)
