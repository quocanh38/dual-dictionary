### Điểm cần nhớ
- stinking rich; stinking rubbish; stinking of smoke
### Dễ nhầm
- stinking is the -ing form of stink and often means smelling very bad; informally it can intensify an adjective, as in stinking rich
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The kitchen was stinking of burnt onions.” (Nhà bếp nồng nặc mùi hành cháy.)
