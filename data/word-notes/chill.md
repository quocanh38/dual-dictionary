### Điểm cần nhớ
- a chill in the air; chill out; chill somebody to the bone
### Dễ nhầm
- chill can be coldness, a relaxed mood or cooling something; chilly is a mild adjective for cold weather
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “There was a chill in the air after sunset.” (Không khí se lạnh sau khi mặt trời lặn.)
