### Điểm cần nhớ
- undercover police officer; go undercover; undercover operation
### Dễ nhầm
- undercover means working secretly to discover information or catch criminals; covert is a more formal synonym
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “An undercover officer joined the investigation.” (Một sĩ quan chìm tham gia cuộc điều tra.)
