### Điểm cần nhớ
- take somebody into custody; child custody; in police custody
### Dễ nhầm
- custody means legal care or control, including detention; prison is a place where convicted people are held
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The suspect was taken into police custody.” (Nghi phạm bị cảnh sát tạm giữ.)
