### Điểm cần nhớ
- dwarf planet; garden dwarf; dwarf → small mythic being/verb make seem small
### Dễ nhầm
- in stories, a dwarf is a small human-like being; in astronomy, dwarf describes a comparatively small type of star or planet; as a verb, dwarf means make something seem small
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Pluto is classified as a dwarf planet.” (Sao Diêm Vương được xếp loại là hành tinh lùn.)
