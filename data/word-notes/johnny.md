### Điểm cần nhớ
- Johnny is a familiar form of John; johnny can mean a condom in informal British slang
### Dễ nhầm
- Capitalize Johnny as a name; the slang noun is informal and context-sensitive
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Johnny called to say he would arrive after lunch.” (Johnny gọi báo sẽ đến sau bữa trưa.)
