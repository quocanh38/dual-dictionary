### Điểm cần nhớ
- revealed the truth; revealed to be; reveal → revealed
### Dễ nhầm
- revealed means made something known or visible; disclosed is more formal and often refers to information
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The survey revealed a change in public opinion.” (Khảo sát cho thấy dư luận đã thay đổi.)
