### Điểm cần nhớ
- the letter H; H-shaped; H-bomb
### Dễ nhầm
- H is the eighth letter; its name is pronounced with an initial vowel sound, “aitch” in British English and often “haitch” in some regions
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The word “hour” begins with H but its first sound is a vowel.” (Từ “hour” bắt đầu bằng H nhưng âm đầu là nguyên âm.)
