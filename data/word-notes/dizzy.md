### Điểm cần nhớ
- get dizzy; dizzy spell; dizzy → feeling the world spin
### Dễ nhầm
- dizzy means feeling as if you or everything around you is spinning; it can also describe a very high height or speed
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “I felt dizzy after standing up too quickly.” (Tôi thấy chóng mặt sau khi đứng dậy quá nhanh.)
