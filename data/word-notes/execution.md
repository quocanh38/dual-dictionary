### Điểm cần nhớ
- carry out an execution; execution of a plan; execute
### Dễ nhầm
- execution can mean carrying out a task or putting someone to death as a legal punishment; implementation refers to putting a plan into effect
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The execution of the project took six months.” (Việc triển khai dự án mất sáu tháng.)
