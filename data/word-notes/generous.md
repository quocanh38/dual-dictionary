### Điểm cần nhớ
- generous with time; generous donation; generosity
### Dễ nhầm
- generous means willing to give or more than is necessary; lavish often describes something abundant or costly
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They made a generous donation to the shelter.” (Họ quyên góp hào phóng cho trung tâm cứu trợ.)
