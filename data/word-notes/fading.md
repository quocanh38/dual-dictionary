### Điểm cần nhớ
- fading light; fading memory; fade → gradually becoming weaker
### Dễ nhầm
- fading is the -ing form of fade and describes colour, sound, memory or strength gradually becoming weaker
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The writing was fading from the old label.” (Chữ viết trên nhãn cũ đang mờ dần.)
