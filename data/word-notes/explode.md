### Điểm cần nhớ
- explode into laughter; explode a bomb; explode → explosion
### Dễ nhầm
- explode means burst violently or increase suddenly; erupt often describes a volcano or a sudden outbreak
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The fireworks seemed to explode above the lake.” (Pháo hoa dường như nổ tung phía trên hồ.)
