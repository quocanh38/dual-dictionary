### Điểm cần nhớ
- amateur photographer; amateur sport; amateur → non-professional
### Dễ nhầm
- an amateur does something for enjoyment rather than as a job; professional does it as paid work or with expert standards
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The festival welcomes amateur musicians.” (Lễ hội chào đón các nhạc sĩ nghiệp dư.)
