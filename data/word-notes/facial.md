### Điểm cần nhớ
- facial expression; facial treatment; facial → relating to the face
### Dễ nhầm
- facial is usually an adjective relating to the face; as a noun, a facial is a beauty treatment for the face
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Her facial expression changed when she heard the news.” (Nét mặt cô thay đổi khi nghe tin.)
