### Điểm cần nhớ
- gin and tonic; gin bottle; gin → juniper-flavoured spirit
### Dễ nhầm
- gin is a strong alcoholic drink made from grain and flavoured with juniper; gin can also name a machine or trap in specialised contexts
### Mẫu câu giá trị
