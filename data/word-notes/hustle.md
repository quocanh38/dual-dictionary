### Điểm cần nhớ
- hustle through a crowd; side hustle; hustle → move quickly or energetic work
### Dễ nhầm
- hustle means move or make someone move quickly; informally, a side hustle is extra paid work, while hustle can also imply dishonest pressure
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She hustled the children onto the bus.” (Cô nhanh chóng đưa bọn trẻ lên xe buýt.)
