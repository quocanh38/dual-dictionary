### Điểm cần nhớ
- drink water/alcohol; drinking fountain; drink → drinking
### Dễ nhầm
- drinking is the -ing form or activity noun; drink can be countable for one serving and uncountable generally
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Avoid drinking from the stream unless the water has been treated.” (Tránh uống nước suối trừ khi nước đã được xử lý.)
