### Điểm cần nhớ
- film trailer; trailer home; trailer → vehicle
### Dễ nhầm
- a trailer is a short advertisement for a film or a vehicle pulled by another vehicle; caravan is a common British term for a movable home
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We watched the trailer before choosing a film.” (Chúng tôi xem đoạn giới thiệu trước khi chọn phim.)
