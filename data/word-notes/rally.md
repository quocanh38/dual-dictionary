### Điểm cần nhớ
- political rally; rally support; rally from an illness
### Dễ nhầm
- a rally is a large public meeting or a recovery; as a verb, rally means gather support or become stronger again
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Thousands attended the election rally.” (Hàng nghìn người dự cuộc mít tinh bầu cử.)
