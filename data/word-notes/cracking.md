### Điểm cần nhớ
- a cracking match; cracking good; cracking → excellent (informal UK)
### Dễ nhầm
- cracking can mean excellent in informal British English; as a noun it can also mean the formation of cracks or a series of sharp sounds
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We had a cracking view from the hill.” (Chúng tôi có tầm nhìn tuyệt đẹp từ ngọn đồi.)
