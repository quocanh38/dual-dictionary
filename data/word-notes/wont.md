### Điểm cần nhớ
- be wont to do something; as is her wont; wont to complain
### Dễ nhầm
- wont means accustomed or likely to do something and is formal or old-fashioned; it is unrelated to won't, the contraction of will not
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He was wont to rise before dawn.” (Ông thường thức dậy trước bình minh.)
