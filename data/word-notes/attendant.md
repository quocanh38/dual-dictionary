### Điểm cần nhớ
- flight attendant; parking attendant; attendant → staff member
### Dễ nhầm
- an attendant is someone who looks after people or a place; assistant is a broad term for someone who helps
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “A flight attendant showed us to our seats.” (Tiếp viên hàng không chỉ chúng tôi đến chỗ ngồi.)
