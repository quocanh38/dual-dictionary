### Điểm cần nhớ
- solve a riddle; speak in riddles; riddle something with holes
### Dễ nhầm
- a riddle is a question or statement requiring clever thought; as a verb it can mean fill something with many holes
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The children tried to solve the riddle.” (Bọn trẻ cố giải câu đố.)
