### Điểm cần nhớ
- herd of cattle; herd instinct; herd → group of animals
### Dễ nhầm
- a herd is a group of animals, especially large grazing animals; as a verb, herd means move or keep a group together
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “A herd of elephants crossed the plain.” (Một đàn voi băng qua đồng bằng.)
