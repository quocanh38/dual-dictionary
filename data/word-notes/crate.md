### Điểm cần nhớ
- crate of bottles; shipping crate; crate → large transport container
### Dễ nhầm
- a crate is a large container used to carry or store goods, often with an open or slatted structure; a box is a broader term
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The workers loaded the crates onto the truck.” (Công nhân chất các thùng hàng lên xe tải.)
