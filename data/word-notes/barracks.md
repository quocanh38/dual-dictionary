### Điểm cần nhớ
- army barracks; barracks room; barracks → military housing
### Dễ nhầm
- barracks are buildings where soldiers live; barrack is often used for a single building, but the plural form is common
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The troops returned to the barracks after training.” (Quân lính trở về doanh trại sau buổi huấn luyện.)
