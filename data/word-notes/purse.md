### Điểm cần nhớ
- purse strings; coin purse; purse → handbag (US usage differs)
### Dễ nhầm
- purse usually means a small bag for money in British English and a handbag in US English; wallet often holds cards and cash
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He found his bus pass in his purse.” (Anh tìm thấy vé xe buýt trong ví.)
