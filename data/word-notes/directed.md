### Điểm cần nhớ
- direct a film; directed at somebody; direct → directed
### Dễ nhầm
- directed means guided or managed, or addressed toward someone; addressed specifically means spoken or written to someone
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The campaign is directed at young voters.” (Chiến dịch hướng đến những cử tri trẻ.)
