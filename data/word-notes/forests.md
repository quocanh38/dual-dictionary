### Điểm cần nhớ
- tropical forests; protect forests; forest → large area of trees
### Dễ nhầm
- forests is the plural of forest, a large area covered mainly with trees; forest can also be a verb meaning cover with trees
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The country is working to protect its forests.” (Quốc gia đang nỗ lực bảo vệ các khu rừng.)
