### Điểm cần nhớ
- tracking hound; hound someone with questions; hound → hunting dog or pursue
### Dễ nhầm
- a hound is a dog bred for hunting by scent or sight; as a verb, hound means pursue or bother someone repeatedly
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The hound followed the scent through the woods.” (Con chó săn lần theo mùi qua khu rừng.)
