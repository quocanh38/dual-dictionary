### Điểm cần nhớ
- doomed to fail; doomed love; doom → certain to suffer/fail
### Dễ nhầm
- doomed describes someone or something that seems certain to fail, suffer or die; doomed to is commonly followed by a verb
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Without support, the plan was doomed to fail.” (Không có sự hỗ trợ, kế hoạch chắc chắn thất bại.)
