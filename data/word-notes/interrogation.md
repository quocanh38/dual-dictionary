### Điểm cần nhớ
- under interrogation; interrogation room; interrogate → formal questioning
### Dễ nhầm
- interrogation is formal, often intense questioning by authorities; interview is a broader and usually less forceful term
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The suspect requested a lawyer before the interrogation.” (Nghi phạm yêu cầu luật sư trước khi thẩm vấn.)
