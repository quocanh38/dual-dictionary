### Điểm cần nhớ
- in the hallway; hallway light; hallway → passage inside a building
### Dễ nhầm
- hallway is a passage inside a building; British English often uses hall or corridor, while hallway is common in North America
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Please leave your shoes in the hallway.” (Vui lòng để giày ngoài hành lang.)
