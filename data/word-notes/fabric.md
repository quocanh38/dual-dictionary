### Điểm cần nhớ
- cotton fabric; social fabric; fabric → cloth or underlying structure
### Dễ nhầm
- fabric means cloth made by weaving or knitting; figuratively, the fabric of society means its underlying structure
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The tailor chose a soft cotton fabric.” (Thợ may chọn một loại vải cotton mềm.)
