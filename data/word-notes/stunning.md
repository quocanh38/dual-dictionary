### Điểm cần nhớ
- stunning view; stunning performance; stunning someone
### Dễ nhầm
- stunning means extremely beautiful or impressive; as a verb form it means shocking or temporarily making someone unconscious
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The mountain view was stunning at sunrise.” (Cảnh núi lúc bình minh đẹp ngoạn mục.)
