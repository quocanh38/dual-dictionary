### Điểm cần nhớ
- hairy arms; hairy situation; hairy → covered with hair or risky
### Dễ nhầm
- hairy means covered with a lot of hair; informally, a hairy situation is difficult or dangerous
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We had a hairy drive through the mountain pass.” (Chúng tôi có chuyến lái xe đầy nguy hiểm qua đèo núi.)
