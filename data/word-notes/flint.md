### Điểm cần nhớ
- flint stone; flint and steel; flint → hard stone making sparks
### Dễ nhầm
- flint is a hard stone that can produce sparks when struck against steel; it was historically used to start fires and make tools
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The guide struck flint against steel to make a spark.” (Hướng dẫn viên đánh đá lửa vào thép để tạo tia lửa.)
