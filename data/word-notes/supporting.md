### Điểm cần nhớ
- supporting evidence; supporting actor; supporting a friend
### Dễ nhầm
- supporting is the -ing form of support; a supporting actor has an important but non-leading role
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She is supporting her friend through a difficult time.” (Cô đang hỗ trợ bạn mình vượt qua giai đoạn khó khăn.)
