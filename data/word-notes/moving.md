### Điểm cần nhớ
- moving house; a moving story; move → moving
### Dễ nhầm
- moving can mean changing location or emotionally affecting; movable describes something that can be moved
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The farewell speech was short but very moving.” (Bài phát biểu chia tay ngắn nhưng rất xúc động.)
