### Điểm cần nhớ
- guidance on a topic; seek guidance; guidance → help or advice
### Dễ nhầm
- guidance is help or advice about what to do and is uncountable; say some guidance, not a guidance
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The students asked for guidance on choosing courses.” (Các sinh viên xin tư vấn về việc chọn môn học.)
