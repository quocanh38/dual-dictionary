### Điểm cần nhớ
- squash a bug; squash into a seat; play squash
### Dễ nhầm
- squash means press or crush something flat, or fit into a small space; squash is also a racket sport and a group of edible gourds
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We had to squash into the back seat.” (Chúng tôi phải chen vào hàng ghế sau.)
