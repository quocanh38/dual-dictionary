### Điểm cần nhớ
- sucking a sweet; sucking noise; suck up to someone
### Dễ nhầm
- sucking is the -ing form of suck, meaning draw liquid or air into the mouth; suck up to someone means flatter them for advantage
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The baby was sucking its thumb.” (Em bé đang mút ngón tay cái.)
