### Điểm cần nhớ
- evidently tired; evidently → clearly/apparently
### Dễ nhầm
- evidently means clearly or apparently based on what can be seen or understood; it can mark an inference rather than certainty
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Evidently, the keys were left inside the car.” (Có vẻ như chìa khóa đã bị bỏ lại trong xe.)
