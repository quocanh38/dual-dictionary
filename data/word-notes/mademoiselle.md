### Điểm cần nhớ
- Bonjour, mademoiselle; Mademoiselle as a French title; plural mesdemoiselles
### Dễ nhầm
- mademoiselle is a French form of address/title traditionally for an unmarried woman; usage is dated or context-sensitive in modern France
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The letter began “Chère Mademoiselle”.” (Bức thư mở đầu bằng “Chère Mademoiselle”.)
