### Điểm cần nhớ
- artificial light; artificial intelligence; artificial → man-made
### Dễ nhầm
- artificial means made by people rather than occurring naturally; synthetic usually refers to materials made through chemical processes
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The room is lit by artificial light.” (Căn phòng được chiếu sáng bằng ánh sáng nhân tạo.)
