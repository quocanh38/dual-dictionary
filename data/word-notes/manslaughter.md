### Điểm cần nhớ
- convicted of manslaughter; involuntary manslaughter; manslaughter charge
### Dễ nhầm
- manslaughter is the unlawful killing of a person without the intent required for murder; legal definitions vary by jurisdiction
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The driver was charged with manslaughter.” (Tài xế bị buộc tội ngộ sát.)
