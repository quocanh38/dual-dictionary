### Điểm cần nhớ
- treated fairly; treated for an infection; treat → treated
### Dễ nhầm
- treated can mean behaved toward someone, gave medical care or dealt with something; cured means made an illness go away
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The patient was treated at the local clinic.” (Bệnh nhân được điều trị tại phòng khám địa phương.)
