### Điểm cần nhớ
- break → broke → broken; broke a promise/window; go broke
### Dễ nhầm
- broke is the simple past of break; broken is the past participle, except broke also informally means having no money
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The handle broke when I tried to open the old door.” (Tay nắm bị gãy khi tôi cố mở cánh cửa cũ.)
