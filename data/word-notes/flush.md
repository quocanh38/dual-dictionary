### Điểm cần nhớ
- flush the toilet; flush with success; flush → send water through/turn red
### Dễ nhầm
- flush can mean send water through a toilet, turn red in the face, or be level with a surface; flush with success means very pleased
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He flushed the toilet after washing his hands.” (Anh xả bồn cầu sau khi rửa tay.)
