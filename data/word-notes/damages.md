### Điểm cần nhớ
- claim damages; pay damages; damage → legal compensation
### Dễ nhầm
- damages is the money awarded by a court as compensation; damage is usually uncountable when it means harm, but damages is a legal plural noun
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The company paid damages to the injured workers.” (Công ty bồi thường cho những công nhân bị thương.)
