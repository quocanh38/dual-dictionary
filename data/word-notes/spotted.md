### Điểm cần nhớ
- spotted a mistake; spotted dress; spot → spotted
### Dễ nhầm
- spotted means noticed something or covered with spots; noticed is a close synonym for seeing something
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She spotted her friend across the crowded room.” (Cô nhận ra bạn mình ở phía bên kia căn phòng đông đúc.)
