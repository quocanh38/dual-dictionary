### Điểm cần nhớ
- shirt cuff; handcuff; cuff → sleeve end
### Dễ nhầm
- a cuff is the turned or finished end of a sleeve; cuffs can also mean handcuffs, and as a verb cuff can mean hit lightly
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He rolled up his cuffs before washing the dishes.” (Anh xắn cổ tay áo trước khi rửa bát.)
