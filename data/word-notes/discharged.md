### Điểm cần nhớ
- discharged from hospital; discharged battery; discharge → released
### Dễ nhầm
- discharged is the past form of discharge; a person may be released, while a battery can be empty of stored power
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She was discharged from hospital after two days.” (Cô được xuất viện sau hai ngày.)
