### Điểm cần nhớ
- selfish behaviour; selfish motives; selfishness
### Dễ nhầm
- selfish means caring mainly about one’s own needs; self-centred is similar but stresses attention to oneself
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “It was selfish to take the last piece without asking.” (Thật ích kỷ khi lấy miếng cuối mà không hỏi.)
