### Điểm cần nhớ
- burned toast; burned out; burn → burned/burnt
### Dễ nhầm
- burned is standard in US English; burnt is common in British English, and both can be past forms
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The toast burned while we were answering the phone.” (Bánh mì bị cháy khi chúng tôi đang nghe điện thoại.)
