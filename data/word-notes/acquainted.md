### Điểm cần nhớ
- be acquainted with somebody; get acquainted; acquaintance
### Dễ nhầm
- acquainted means knowing someone or being familiar with something; familiar with usually refers to knowledge rather than a personal relationship
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Are you acquainted with the new manager?” (Bạn có quen người quản lý mới không?)
