### Điểm cần nhớ
- box cutter; pastry cutter; cutter → tool that cuts
### Dễ nhầm
- a cutter is a tool or machine for cutting, or a person who cuts something; in compounds, the first word often specifies its use
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Use a pastry cutter to shape the dough.” (Dùng khuôn cắt bánh để tạo hình cho bột.)
