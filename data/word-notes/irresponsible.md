### Điểm cần nhớ
- irresponsible behaviour; irresponsible for; irresponsible → not acting with care
### Dễ nhầm
- irresponsible describes someone who does not consider the likely effects of their actions; it is strongly critical
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Leaving the door unlocked was irresponsible.” (Để cửa không khóa là hành động thiếu trách nhiệm.)
