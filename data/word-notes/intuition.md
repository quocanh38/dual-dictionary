### Điểm cần nhớ
- trust your intuition; act on intuition; intuition → knowing without conscious reasoning
### Dễ nhầm
- intuition is the ability to understand or know something without consciously reasoning it out; gut feeling is an informal equivalent
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Her intuition told her something was wrong.” (Trực giác mách bảo cô rằng có điều gì đó không ổn.)
