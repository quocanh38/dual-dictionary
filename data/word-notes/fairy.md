### Điểm cần nhớ
- fairy tale; fairy godmother; fairy ring
### Dễ nhầm
- a fairy is a small imaginary being with magical powers; elf is another mythical being with different traditions
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The child dressed as a fairy for the party.” (Đứa trẻ hóa trang thành nàng tiên trong bữa tiệc.)
