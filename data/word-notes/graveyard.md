### Điểm cần nhớ
- old graveyard; graveyard shift; graveyard → burial ground
### Dễ nhầm
- a graveyard is an area where people are buried, often beside a church; graveyard shift is an informal term for a night work shift
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The old graveyard stood behind the church.” (Nghĩa trang cũ nằm phía sau nhà thờ.)
