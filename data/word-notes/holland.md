### Điểm cần nhớ
- Holland; the Netherlands; Holland → common name for the Netherlands
### Dễ nhầm
- Holland is often used in English for the Netherlands, but technically names only North Holland and South Holland; use the Netherlands for the whole country
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They travelled through the Netherlands, including North Holland.” (Họ đi khắp Hà Lan, trong đó có tỉnh Bắc Holland.)
