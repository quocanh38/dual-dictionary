### Điểm cần nhớ
- chase after/follow somebody; chase a dream; police chase
### Dễ nhầm
- chase is to pursue in order to catch or reach; follow does not necessarily imply trying to catch
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The dog chased a ball across the garden.” (Con chó đuổi theo quả bóng băng qua khu vườn.)
