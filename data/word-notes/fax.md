### Điểm cần nhớ
- send by fax; fax machine; fax → transmit a document over a phone line
### Dễ nhầm
- fax is a machine or system for sending copies of documents over a telephone line; it can also be the verb for sending one
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The clinic sent the report by fax.” (Phòng khám gửi báo cáo bằng fax.)
