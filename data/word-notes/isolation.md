### Điểm cần nhớ
- in isolation; social isolation; isolation → separation from others
### Dễ nhầm
- isolation is the state or process of being separated from other people or things; in isolation can mean alone or considered separately
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The patient was kept in isolation to prevent infection.” (Bệnh nhân được cách ly để ngăn lây nhiễm.)
