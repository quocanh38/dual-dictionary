### Điểm cần nhớ
- make suggestions; helpful suggestions; suggestion box
### Dễ nhầm
- suggestions is the plural of suggestion; a suggestion is an idea offered for consideration, usually less forceful than advice
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We welcome suggestions from our readers.” (Chúng tôi hoan nghênh góp ý từ độc giả.)
