### Điểm cần nhớ
- attending physician; consult a physician; physician's office
### Dễ nhầm
- physician is a formal word for a doctor, especially one who practices medicine; it does not usually mean a surgeon specifically
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The physician recommended a follow-up visit.” (Bác sĩ khuyên nên tái khám.)
