### Điểm cần nhớ
- play the guitar; guitar lesson/string; guitar is often used with the
### Dễ nhầm
- English commonly uses play the guitar with an article; the instrument name alone is not usually used as a bare object in this pattern
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He learned to play the guitar from online lessons.” (Anh học chơi đàn guitar qua các bài học trực tuyến.)
