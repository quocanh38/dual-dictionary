### Điểm cần nhớ
- jingle of keys; advertising jingle; jingle → light ringing sound
### Dễ nhầm
- a jingle is a short light ringing sound or a brief catchy song used in advertising; as a verb it means make that sound
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The keys jingled in his pocket.” (Chùm chìa khóa leng keng trong túi anh.)
