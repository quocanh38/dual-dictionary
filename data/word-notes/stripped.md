### Điểm cần nhớ
- stripped bare; stripped of rights; stripped the paint
### Dễ nhầm
- stripped is the past form of strip, meaning remove a covering or take something away; strip someone of rights is formal
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The old wallpaper was stripped from the walls.” (Lớp giấy dán tường cũ đã được bóc khỏi tường.)
