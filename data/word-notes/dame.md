### Điểm cần nhớ
- Dame title; Dame Judi Dench; dame → female honour title
### Dễ nhầm
- in the UK, Dame is a title awarded to a woman for achievement; it is capitalized before a name and is not the same as dame meaning a woman in older usage
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She was appointed a Dame for her work in science.” (Bà được phong tước Dame nhờ những đóng góp trong khoa học.)
