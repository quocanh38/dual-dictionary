### Điểm cần nhớ
- maternal/paternal grandfather; grandfather clock; grandfather a rule
### Dễ nhầm
- grandfather is a parent’s father; grandfather can also be a verb in grandfather clause contexts
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “My grandfather taught me to repair old radios.” (Ông tôi dạy tôi sửa radio cũ.)
