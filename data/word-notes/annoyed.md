### Điểm cần nhớ
- feel annoyed; annoyed with somebody; annoy → annoyed
### Dễ nhầm
- annoyed describes someone feeling slightly angry or impatient; annoying describes what causes the feeling
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She was annoyed by the constant interruptions.” (Cô khó chịu vì liên tục bị ngắt lời.)
