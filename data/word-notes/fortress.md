### Điểm cần nhớ
- medieval fortress; fortress walls; fortress → strongly protected place
### Dễ nhầm
- a fortress is a building or place made strong to defend against attack; figuratively, a well-protected organisation can be called a fortress
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The fortress overlooked the harbour.” (Pháo đài nhìn xuống bến cảng.)
