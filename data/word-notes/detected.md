### Điểm cần nhớ
- detected a signal; early-detected disease; detect → noticed/discovered
### Dễ nhầm
- detected is the past form of detect, meaning discovered or noticed something that was not immediately obvious
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The sensor detected smoke in the hallway.” (Cảm biến phát hiện khói trong hành lang.)
