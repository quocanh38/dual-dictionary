### Điểm cần nhớ
- Jaws; the jaws of a trap; jaws → plural of jaw or film title
### Dễ nhầm
- Jaws is the title of the 1975 shark film; in ordinary use, jaws is the plural of jaw or the gripping parts of a tool
### Mẫu câu giá trị
