### Điểm cần nhớ
- drive on the freeway; freeway exit; freeway → motorway (UK)
### Dễ nhầm
- freeway is North American English for a major road with controlled access; British English commonly says motorway
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Traffic was moving slowly on the freeway.” (Giao thông di chuyển chậm trên đường cao tốc.)
