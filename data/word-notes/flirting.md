### Điểm cần nhớ
- flirting with a colleague; flirting with danger; flirt → flirting
### Dễ nhầm
- flirting is the -ing form of flirt; it can mean playful romantic behaviour or taking a risk in flirt with danger
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She thought he was flirting, but he was only being friendly.” (Cô nghĩ anh đang tán tỉnh, nhưng anh chỉ thân thiện.)
