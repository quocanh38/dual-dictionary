### Điểm cần nhớ
- cancelled flight; cancelled a meeting; cancel → cancelled
### Dễ nhầm
- cancelled is the usual British spelling of canceled; both mean decided that an event will not happen
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The concert was cancelled because of heavy rain.” (Buổi hòa nhạc bị hủy vì mưa lớn.)
