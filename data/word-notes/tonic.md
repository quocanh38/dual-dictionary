### Điểm cần nhớ
- tonic water; tonic for the nerves; tonic-clonic seizure
### Dễ nhầm
- tonic can mean invigorating or a medicine intended to restore health; tonic water is a carbonated drink with quinine
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He ordered gin and tonic at the bar.” (Anh gọi rượu gin pha nước tonic ở quầy bar.)
