### Điểm cần nhớ
- covered wagon; wagon wheel; wagon → cart
### Dễ nhầm
- a wagon is a vehicle with four wheels, usually pulled by an animal or attached to a car; cart is often smaller
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The children rode in a wagon around the park.” (Bọn trẻ ngồi trên xe kéo đi quanh công viên.)
