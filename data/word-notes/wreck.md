### Điểm cần nhớ
- shipwreck; car wreck; wreck somebody’s plans
### Dễ nhầm
- a wreck is a badly damaged vehicle or ship, or a verb meaning destroy; crash is the collision, while wreck is the resulting damage
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The storm left the fishing boat a wreck.” (Cơn bão khiến thuyền đánh cá bị phá hỏng.)
