### Điểm cần nhớ
- a welcome distraction; distraction from work; distraction → something taking attention away
### Dễ nhầm
- a distraction is something that takes attention away, sometimes unpleasantly and sometimes as a welcome break
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “A short walk was a welcome distraction.” (Một chuyến đi bộ ngắn là khoảng nghỉ dễ chịu.)
