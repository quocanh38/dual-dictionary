### Điểm cần nhớ
- intimate friends; intimate details; intimate → close/private
### Dễ nhầm
- as an adjective, intimate means very close or private; as a verb, intimate means suggest something indirectly and is more formal
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They shared an intimate conversation after dinner.” (Họ có cuộc trò chuyện riêng tư sau bữa tối.)
