### Điểm cần nhớ
- exotic flowers; exotic destination; exotic → strikingly unfamiliar
### Dễ nhầm
- exotic describes something from another country, especially a tropical one, or something unusually different; it depends on the speaker's perspective
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The garden was filled with exotic plants.” (Khu vườn đầy những loài cây lạ.)
