### Điểm cần nhớ
- defensive position; get defensive; defensive → protecting/self-protective
### Dễ nhầm
- defensive means intended to protect against attack; a person can also become defensive when they feel criticised
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She became defensive when asked about the mistake.” (Cô ấy trở nên phòng thủ khi được hỏi về sai sót.)
