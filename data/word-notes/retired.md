### Điểm cần nhớ
- retired from teaching; retired life; retire → retired
### Dễ nhầm
- retired means having stopped working permanently, usually because of age; resigned means voluntarily left a job
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “My grandfather retired at sixty-five.” (Ông tôi nghỉ hưu ở tuổi sáu mươi lăm.)
