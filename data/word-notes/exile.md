### Điểm cần nhớ
- live in exile; send someone into exile; exile → forced absence from home
### Dễ nhầm
- exile is the state of being forced to live away from one's home or country; as a verb it means force someone to leave
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The writer spent years in exile abroad.” (Nhà văn sống lưu vong ở nước ngoài nhiều năm.)
