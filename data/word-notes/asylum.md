### Điểm cần nhớ
- seek asylum; asylum seeker; asylum → protection/refuge
### Dễ nhầm
- asylum is protection granted by a country to someone fleeing danger or persecution; refugee is a person who has fled their country
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She applied for asylum after leaving her home country.” (Cô xin tị nạn sau khi rời quê hương.)
