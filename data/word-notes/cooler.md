### Điểm cần nhớ
- a cooler full of drinks; water cooler; cooler → cool box
### Dễ nhầm
- a cooler is a device or insulated container that keeps things cool; in North American English it commonly means a portable drinks container
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We packed a cooler with water for the drive.” (Chúng tôi xếp nước vào thùng giữ lạnh cho chuyến đi.)
