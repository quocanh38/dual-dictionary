### Điểm cần nhớ
- Countess of; Earl and Countess; countess → female noble title
### Dễ nhầm
- a countess is a woman holding the rank corresponding to a count or earl, or the wife of one; it is a title rather than a synonym for queen
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The countess welcomed the guests to her home.” (Nữ bá tước đón khách tại nhà.)
