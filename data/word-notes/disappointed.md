### Điểm cần nhớ
- be disappointed with something; disappointed to hear; disappoint → disappointed
### Dễ nhầm
- disappointed describes sadness because something was worse than hoped; disappointing describes the thing that caused it
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We were disappointed by the cancelled concert.” (Chúng tôi thất vọng vì buổi hòa nhạc bị hủy.)
