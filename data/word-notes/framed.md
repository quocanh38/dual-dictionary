### Điểm cần nhớ
- framed photograph; framed for a crime; frame → put in a frame or falsely accuse
### Dễ nhầm
- framed can mean placed in a frame or falsely made to appear guilty; frame someone for a crime is an idiom for setting them up
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The suspect claimed he had been framed.” (Nghi phạm khẳng định mình bị gài bẫy.)
