### Điểm cần nhớ
- forged document; forged a key; forge → made or copied falsely
### Dễ nhầm
- forged is the past form of forge; a forged document is falsely made, while a forged friendship is built through effort
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The bank rejected the forged cheque.” (Ngân hàng từ chối tấm séc giả mạo.)
