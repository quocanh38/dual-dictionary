### Điểm cần nhớ
- speaking English; generally speaking; public speaking; speak → speaking
### Dễ nhầm
- speaking is the -ing form of speak and appears in phrases such as speaking of
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Generally speaking, the coastal route is less busy.” (Nói chung, tuyến đường ven biển ít đông hơn.)
