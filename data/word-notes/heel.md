### Điểm cần nhớ
- heel of your foot; shoe heel; heel → back part of foot/shoe
### Dễ nhầm
- the heel is the back part of the foot or the raised part of a shoe; heel of a loaf is the end slice
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The shoes rubbed against the back of my heel.” (Đôi giày cọ vào phía sau gót chân tôi.)
