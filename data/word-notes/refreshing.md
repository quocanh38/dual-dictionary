### Điểm cần nhớ
- refreshing drink; refreshing change; refreshingly honest
### Dễ nhầm
- refreshing means making someone feel less tired or pleasantly different; it is the -ing form of refresh
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The cool water was refreshing after the hike.” (Nước mát thật sảng khoái sau chuyến đi bộ.)
