### Điểm cần nhớ
- hurricane warning; hurricane season; hurricane → violent tropical storm
### Dễ nhầm
- a hurricane is a violent tropical storm with very strong winds; the same type of storm is called a typhoon or cyclone in other regions
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The hurricane forced residents to leave the coast.” (Cơn bão buộc cư dân phải rời khỏi bờ biển.)
