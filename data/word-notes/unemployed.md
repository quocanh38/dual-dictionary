### Điểm cần nhớ
- unemployed people; become unemployed; long-term unemployed
### Dễ nhầm
- unemployed means not having a paid job but looking for work; it differs from economically inactive
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She was unemployed for three months after graduation.” (Cô thất nghiệp ba tháng sau khi tốt nghiệp.)
