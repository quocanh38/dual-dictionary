### Điểm cần nhớ
- wild hog; hog the conversation; hog → pig or selfishly take
### Dễ nhầm
- hog is especially North American for a pig, particularly one raised for meat; as a verb, hog means take more than one's share
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Please don't hog all the space on the table.” (Đừng chiếm hết chỗ trên bàn nhé.)
