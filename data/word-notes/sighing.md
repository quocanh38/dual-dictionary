### Điểm cần nhớ
- sighing with relief; sighing deeply; sigh → sighing
### Dễ nhầm
- sighing is breathing out slowly to express tiredness, sadness or relief; groaning is a lower sound often showing pain
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She was sighing with relief after the exam.” (Cô thở phào nhẹ nhõm sau kỳ thi.)
