### Điểm cần nhớ
- be supposed to; supposed that; supposedly
### Dễ nhầm
- be supposed to expresses expectation, duty or what people believe; supposed alone is not always a passive form
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The package is supposed to arrive before noon.” (Gói hàng dự kiến sẽ đến trước buổi trưa.)
