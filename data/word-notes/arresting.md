### Điểm cần nhớ
- arresting a suspect; arresting image; arrest → arresting
### Dễ nhầm
- arresting can mean taking someone into police custody or being striking and attention-grabbing; striking is a close adjective synonym
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The painting has an arresting use of colour.” (Bức tranh sử dụng màu sắc rất bắt mắt.)
