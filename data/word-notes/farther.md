### Điểm cần nhớ
- farther away; farther down the road; farther → greater physical distance
### Dễ nhầm
- farther is the comparative of far and often refers to physical distance; further is also used for distance and is more common for figurative meaning
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We walked farther than we had planned.” (Chúng tôi đi xa hơn dự định.)
