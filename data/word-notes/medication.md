### Điểm cần nhớ
- take medication; medication dosage; medicate
### Dễ nhầm
- medication is a medicine or the act of treating someone with medicine; medicine can also refer broadly to the science of treating illness
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He takes medication for high blood pressure.” (Anh dùng thuốc điều trị huyết áp cao.)
