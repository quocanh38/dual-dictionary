### Điểm cần nhớ
- rebel against authority; rebel leader; rebel group
### Dễ nhầm
- rebel as a noun is a person who resists authority; as a verb the stress shifts to the second syllable: reBEL
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The rebels took control of the town.” (Phe nổi dậy giành quyền kiểm soát thị trấn.)
