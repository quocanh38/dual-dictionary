### Điểm cần nhớ
- sewing thread; thread a needle; online thread
### Dễ nhầm
- a thread is a thin strand used for sewing or a series of connected messages or ideas; as a verb it means pass through a narrow opening
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She threaded the needle carefully.” (Cô cẩn thận xỏ chỉ qua kim.)
