### Điểm cần nhớ
- get lost in a maze; hedge maze; maze of streets
### Dễ nhầm
- a maze is a network of paths that is difficult to find your way through; figuratively it can mean a confusing system
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The children ran through the hedge maze.” (Bọn trẻ chạy qua mê cung hàng rào cây.)
