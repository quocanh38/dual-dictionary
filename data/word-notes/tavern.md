### Điểm cần nhớ
- old tavern; tavern keeper; country tavern
### Dễ nhầm
- a tavern is an old-fashioned word for a place that serves drinks and sometimes food; pub is more common in everyday British English
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They stopped at a tavern for dinner.” (Họ ghé một quán rượu để ăn tối.)
