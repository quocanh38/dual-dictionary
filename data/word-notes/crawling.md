### Điểm cần nhớ
- crawling with insects; crawling under; crawl → crawling
### Dễ nhầm
- crawling is the -ing form of crawl and can describe slow movement; crawling with something means covered or full of it
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The path was crawling with ants after the rain.” (Con đường đầy kiến sau cơn mưa.)
