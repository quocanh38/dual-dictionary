### Điểm cần nhớ
- whilst can mean “while”; common in British English and more formal than everyday while
### Dễ nhầm
- whilst is a conjunction, not a noun; in most contexts while sounds more natural in conversation
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Whilst the paint was drying, we cleaned the brushes.” (Trong lúc chờ sơn khô, chúng tôi rửa cọ.)
