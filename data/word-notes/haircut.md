### Điểm cần nhớ
- get a haircut; haircut style; haircut → cutting or style of hair
### Dễ nhầm
- a haircut is the act of cutting hair or the style produced; give someone a haircut means cut their hair
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He got a short haircut before the interview.” (Anh cắt tóc ngắn trước buổi phỏng vấn.)
