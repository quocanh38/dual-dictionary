### Điểm cần nhớ
- take the ferry; ferry service; ferry → boat carrying people or vehicles
### Dễ nhầm
- a ferry is a boat or ship that carries people, vehicles or goods on a regular route; ferry can also be a verb meaning transport them
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We took the ferry across the bay.” (Chúng tôi đi phà qua vịnh.)
