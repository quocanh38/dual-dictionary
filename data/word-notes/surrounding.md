### Điểm cần nhớ
- surrounding area; surrounding countryside; surrounding circumstances
### Dễ nhầm
- surrounding is the -ing form of surround or an adjective meaning nearby; surroundings is the noun for the environment
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The surrounding hills were covered in snow.” (Những ngọn đồi xung quanh phủ đầy tuyết.)
