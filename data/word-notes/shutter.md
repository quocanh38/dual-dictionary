### Điểm cần nhớ
- window shutter; camera shutter; shutter a factory
### Dễ nhầm
- a shutter is a cover for a window or the part of a camera controlling exposure; as a verb it can mean close a business
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They closed the shutters before the storm.” (Họ đóng cửa chớp trước cơn bão.)
