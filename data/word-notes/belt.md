### Điểm cần nhớ
- wear a belt; seat belt; belt around the waist; belt out a song
### Dễ nhầm
- belt can mean a strap, a geographic zone or (informally) sing loudly; belt is not limited to clothing
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Fasten your seat belt before the car moves.” (Hãy thắt dây an toàn trước khi xe chạy.)
