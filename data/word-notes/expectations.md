### Điểm cần nhớ
- meet expectations; high expectations; expectation → belief about what will happen
### Dễ nhầm
- expectations is the plural of expectation, a belief or standard about what should happen; meet expectations means be as good as expected
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The results exceeded our expectations.” (Kết quả vượt quá mong đợi của chúng tôi.)
