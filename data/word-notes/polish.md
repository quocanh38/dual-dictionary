### Điểm cần nhớ
- polish your shoes; nail polish; Polish language
### Dễ nhầm
- polish as a verb means make something smooth or shiny; Polish with a capital P refers to Poland or its language
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He polished the silver until it shone.” (Anh đánh bóng bạc cho đến khi nó sáng lên.)
