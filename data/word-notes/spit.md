### Điểm cần nhớ
- spit out food; spit in somebody’s face; spit → saliva
### Dễ nhầm
- spit means force saliva out of the mouth or, as a noun, the saliva itself; expectorate is the formal medical verb
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The child was told not to spit on the pavement.” (Đứa trẻ được nhắc không nhổ nước bọt xuống vỉa hè.)
