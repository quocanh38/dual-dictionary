### Điểm cần nhớ
- donkey's ears; donkey work; donkey → animal of the horse family
### Dễ nhầm
- a donkey is a small member of the horse family with long ears; donkey work informally means boring, tiring work
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The donkey carried supplies up the hill.” (Con lừa chở đồ tiếp tế lên đồi.)
