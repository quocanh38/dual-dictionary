### Điểm cần nhớ
- Buddhist monk; monk’s robe; monk → religious man
### Dễ nhầm
- a monk is a man who belongs to a religious community and follows its rules; a nun is the female equivalent in many traditions
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The monk meditated quietly in the temple.” (Nhà sư lặng lẽ thiền định trong chùa.)
