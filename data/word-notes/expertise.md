### Điểm cần nhớ
- technical expertise; expertise in; expertise → expert skill or knowledge
### Dễ nhầm
- expertise is expert knowledge or skill in a subject and is uncountable; say a lot of expertise, not an expertise
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The project needs expertise in cybersecurity.” (Dự án cần chuyên môn về an ninh mạng.)
