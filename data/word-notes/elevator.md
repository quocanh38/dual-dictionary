### Điểm cần nhớ
- take the elevator; elevator music; elevator → lift (UK)
### Dễ nhầm
- elevator is the US word for a device that carries people between floors; lift is common in British English
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We took the elevator to the tenth floor.” (Chúng tôi đi thang máy lên tầng mười.)
