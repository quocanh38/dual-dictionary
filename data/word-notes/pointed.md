### Điểm cần nhớ
- pointed question; pointed remark; pointed shoe
### Dễ nhầm
- pointed can mean having a sharp end or deliberately direct and critical; it is also the past form of point
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He made a pointed comment about the delay.” (Anh đưa ra lời nhận xét gay gắt về sự chậm trễ.)
