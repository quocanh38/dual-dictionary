### Điểm cần nhớ
- preach a sermon; preach to somebody; preach about tolerance
### Dễ nhầm
- preach means deliver a religious message or strongly advise people; preach to someone can imply unwanted moralizing
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The minister preached about compassion.” (Mục sư giảng về lòng trắc ẩn.)
