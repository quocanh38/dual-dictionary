### Điểm cần nhớ
- accompanied by music; accompanied a child; accompany → accompanied
### Dễ nhầm
- accompanied means went with someone or happened together with something; escorted suggests guiding or protecting a person
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The singer was accompanied by a pianist.” (Ca sĩ được một nghệ sĩ piano đệm đàn.)
