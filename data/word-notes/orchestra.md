### Điểm cần nhớ
- symphony orchestra; orchestra pit; play in an orchestra
### Dễ nhầm
- an orchestra is a large group of musicians, especially playing classical music; a band usually has a different mix of instruments
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She plays violin in the orchestra.” (Cô chơi vĩ cầm trong dàn nhạc.)
