### Điểm cần nhớ
- massacre civilians; a massacre of protesters; massacre the opposition
### Dễ nhầm
- massacre means kill many people violently; as a verb, it can also mean defeat an opponent very badly in informal use
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The massacre shocked the entire region.” (Vụ thảm sát khiến cả khu vực bàng hoàng.)
