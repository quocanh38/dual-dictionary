### Điểm cần nhớ
- anytime you need help; call anytime; informal “you’re welcome”
### Dễ nhầm
- anytime as one word is an adverb meaning at any time; any time as two words is a noun phrase
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “You can contact the support desk anytime during opening hours.” (Bạn có thể liên hệ quầy hỗ trợ bất cứ lúc nào trong giờ mở cửa.)
