### Điểm cần nhớ
- shepherd a flock; shepherd somebody through a process; shepherd's pie
### Dễ nhầm
- a shepherd looks after sheep; as a verb, shepherd means guide or manage people through a process
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The guide shepherded the visitors through the gallery.” (Hướng dẫn viên dẫn khách tham quan qua phòng tranh.)
