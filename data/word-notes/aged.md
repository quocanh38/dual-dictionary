### Điểm cần nhớ
- aged five; aged wine; age → aged
### Dễ nhầm
- aged can mean having lived for a stated number of years, or matured over time; elderly is used for people who are old
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The programme is for children aged six to ten.” (Chương trình dành cho trẻ từ sáu đến mười tuổi.)
