### Điểm cần nhớ
- stand-up comedian; comedian’s joke; comedy
### Dễ nhầm
- a comedian performs jokes or funny material; comic is a related term that can also mean a comedy performer
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The comedian had the audience laughing all evening.” (Diễn viên hài khiến khán giả cười suốt buổi tối.)
