### Điểm cần nhớ
- military invasion; invasion of privacy; invade → act of entering by force
### Dễ nhầm
- an invasion is a forceful entry into a country or an unwelcome intrusion into someone's privacy or space
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The court ruled that the recording was an invasion of privacy.” (Tòa phán quyết rằng việc ghi âm là hành vi xâm phạm quyền riêng tư.)
