### Điểm cần nhớ
- crawl on hands and knees; traffic crawls; crawl → move slowly/close to ground
### Dễ nhầm
- to crawl is to move close to the ground, often on hands and knees; traffic can also crawl when it moves very slowly
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The baby began to crawl across the rug.” (Em bé bắt đầu bò qua tấm thảm.)
