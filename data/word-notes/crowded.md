### Điểm cần nhớ
- crowded streets; crowded with people; crowded → full of people
### Dễ nhầm
- crowded means containing many people, sometimes too many; a schedule can also be crowded when it has many activities
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The bus was crowded with commuters.” (Xe buýt đông nghịt người đi làm.)
