### Điểm cần nhớ
- passing traffic; passing an exam; pass → passing
### Dễ nhầm
- passing can mean going by, succeeding in a test or the act of passing; passing away is a gentle expression for dying
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She celebrated passing her driving test.” (Cô ăn mừng vì vượt qua kỳ thi lái xe.)
