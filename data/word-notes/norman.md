### Điểm cần nhớ
- Norman architecture; Norman (given name); Norman → proper name/adjective
### Dễ nhầm
- Norman can refer to the people who conquered England in 1066, their style, or a personal name; capitalize it in these uses
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The castle was built in the Norman style.” (Lâu đài được xây theo phong cách Norman.)
