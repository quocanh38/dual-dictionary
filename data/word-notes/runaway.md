### Điểm cần nhớ
- runaway train; runaway success; runaway from home
### Dễ nhầm
- runaway can be a person who has run away or something moving out of control; as an adjective it often means uncontrolled
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The book became a runaway bestseller.” (Cuốn sách trở thành sách bán chạy vượt trội.)
