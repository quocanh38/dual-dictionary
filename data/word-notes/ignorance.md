### Điểm cần nhớ
- ignorance of the rules; admit ignorance; ignorance → lack of knowledge
### Dễ nhầm
- ignorance is a lack of knowledge about something and is uncountable; it does not necessarily mean lack of intelligence
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He admitted his ignorance of the local customs.” (Anh thừa nhận mình không biết phong tục địa phương.)
