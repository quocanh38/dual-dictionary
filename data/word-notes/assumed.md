### Điểm cần nhớ
- assumed that; assumed responsibility; assume → assumed
### Dễ nhầm
- assumed means accepted as true or took on a role; presumed is similar but often more formal
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We assumed the road was open because no sign was posted.” (Chúng tôi cho rằng đường mở vì không có biển báo.)
