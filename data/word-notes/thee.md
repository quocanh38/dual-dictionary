### Điểm cần nhớ
- I give it to thee; thee → archaic you (object)
### Dễ nhầm
- thee is an archaic form of you used as an object, especially in older religious or poetic language; you is standard today
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The poem says, “I will remember thee.”” (Bài thơ viết: “Ta sẽ nhớ đến người.”)
