### Điểm cần nhớ
- dialing a number; speed dialing; dial → dialing/dialling
### Dễ nhầm
- dialing is the usual North American spelling of dialling; it means entering a phone number or adjusting a dial
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He was dialing the wrong number repeatedly.” (Anh ấy liên tục bấm nhầm số.)
