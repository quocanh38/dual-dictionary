### Điểm cần nhớ
- my darling; darling child; darling! as a warm form of address
### Dễ nhầm
- darling can be affectionate or, in some contexts, patronizing; dear is another form of address
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Good night, darling. I’ll see you in the morning.” (Ngủ ngon nhé, em yêu. Sáng mai gặp lại.)
