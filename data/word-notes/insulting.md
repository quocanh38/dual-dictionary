### Điểm cần nhớ
- insulting remark; find something insulting; insult → offensive
### Dễ nhầm
- insulting means rude or likely to offend someone's dignity; an insult is a remark or action that does this
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He apologised for the insulting comment.” (Anh xin lỗi vì lời nhận xét xúc phạm.)
