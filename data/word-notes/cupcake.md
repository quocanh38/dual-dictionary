### Điểm cần nhớ
- vanilla cupcake; cupcake case; cupcake → small individual cake
### Dễ nhầm
- a cupcake is a small cake baked in a paper cup; British English may also use fairy cake for a similar small cake
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He brought a box of cupcakes to the party.” (Anh mang một hộp bánh cupcake đến bữa tiệc.)
