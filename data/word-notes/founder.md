### Điểm cần nhớ
- company founder; founding members; founder → person who starts
### Dễ nhầm
- a founder is someone who starts an organisation or institution; co-founder means one of two or more people who start it together
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The founder still advises the company.” (Người sáng lập vẫn cố vấn cho công ty.)
