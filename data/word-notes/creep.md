### Điểm cần nhớ
- creep quietly; creep up on; creep → move stealthily
### Dễ nhầm
- to creep is to move slowly and quietly, often to avoid being seen; its past forms are crept, not creeped, except in some phrasal uses such as creep someone out
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She crept downstairs so she would not wake anyone.” (Cô lẻn xuống cầu thang để không đánh thức ai.)
