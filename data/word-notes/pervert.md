### Điểm cần nhớ
- sexual pervert; pervert the truth; perverted meaning
### Dễ nhầm
- pervert as a noun is a strongly disapproving label for someone whose sexual behaviour is considered abnormal or harmful; as a verb it means distort or corrupt
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The report accused him of trying to pervert the truth.” (Báo cáo cáo buộc anh ta tìm cách bóp méo sự thật.)
