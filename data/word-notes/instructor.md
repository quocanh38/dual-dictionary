### Điểm cần nhớ
- driving instructor; ski instructor; instructor → teacher of a practical skill
### Dễ nhầm
- an instructor teaches a practical skill or activity, such as driving or skiing; teacher is broader
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The driving instructor asked him to check the mirrors.” (Giáo viên dạy lái xe yêu cầu anh kiểm tra gương.)
