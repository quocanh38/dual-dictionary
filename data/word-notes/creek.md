### Điểm cần nhớ
- small creek; creek bank; creek → stream/inlet
### Dễ nhầm
- creek means a small stream in North American English; in British English it can mean a narrow inlet where the sea reaches inland
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We followed the creek through the woods.” (Chúng tôi men theo con suối xuyên qua khu rừng.)
