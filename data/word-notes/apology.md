### Điểm cần nhớ
- make/offer an apology; apology for something; apologize
### Dễ nhầm
- apology is the noun; apologize/apologise is the verb; make an apology is more formal than say sorry
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The company issued an apology for the delayed delivery.” (Công ty đưa ra lời xin lỗi vì giao hàng chậm.)
