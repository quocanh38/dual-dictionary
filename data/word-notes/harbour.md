### Điểm cần nhớ
- safe harbour; harbour a suspicion; harbour → sheltered water or shelter
### Dễ nhầm
- harbour is the British spelling of harbor; it can mean a sheltered place for ships or, as a verb, give shelter or keep a thought
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The fishing boats returned to the harbour before the storm.” (Những chiếc thuyền đánh cá trở về cảng trước cơn bão.)
