### Điểm cần nhớ
- sports facilities; toilet facilities; facility → services or equipment provided
### Dễ nhầm
- facilities is the plural of facility and often means buildings, equipment or services provided for a purpose; a facility can also mean an ability
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The hotel has excellent conference facilities.” (Khách sạn có cơ sở vật chất hội nghị rất tốt.)
