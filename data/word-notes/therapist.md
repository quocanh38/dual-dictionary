### Điểm cần nhớ
- physical therapist; family therapist; therapy
### Dễ nhầm
- a therapist is a trained professional who provides a particular kind of treatment or support; a therapist is not necessarily a medical doctor
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The therapist helped him recover after the injury.” (Nhà trị liệu giúp anh hồi phục sau chấn thương.)
