### Điểm cần nhớ
- dramatic irony; the irony is; irony → contrast between expectation and reality
### Dễ nhầm
- irony is a contrast between what is expected and what actually happens, or language meaning the opposite of its literal words; coincidence alone is not always irony
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The irony was that the rain began after they bought umbrellas.” (Trớ trêu là mưa bắt đầu sau khi họ mua ô.)
