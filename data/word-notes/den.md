### Điểm cần nhớ
- bear's den; a den of thieves; den → animal's hidden home
### Dễ nhầm
- a den is the hidden home of a wild animal; figuratively, a den of thieves is a place associated with dishonest people
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The fox returned to its den before dawn.” (Con cáo trở về hang trước bình minh.)
