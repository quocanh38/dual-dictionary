### Điểm cần nhớ
- feel frustrated; frustrated by delays; frustrate → feeling blocked
### Dễ nhầm
- frustrated describes someone annoyed because they cannot achieve or do something; frustrating describes the thing causing the feeling
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She felt frustrated by the repeated delays.” (Cô bực bội vì những lần trì hoãn liên tiếp.)
