### Điểm cần nhớ
- drunken driver; drunken argument; drunken → caused by being drunk
### Dễ nhầm
- drunken is usually used before a noun and means drunk or involving someone who has been drinking alcohol; drunk is more common after a verb
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “A drunken passenger caused a disturbance.” (Một hành khách say rượu gây náo loạn.)
