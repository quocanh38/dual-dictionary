### Điểm cần nhớ
- impatient with delays; impatient to begin; impatient → unable to wait calmly
### Dễ nhầm
- impatient describes someone annoyed by waiting or wanting something to happen soon; impatient with names what causes the feeling
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The children grew impatient during the long wait.” (Bọn trẻ trở nên sốt ruột trong lúc chờ đợi lâu.)
