### Điểm cần nhớ
- aid convoy; military convoy; convoy → vehicles travelling together
### Dễ nhầm
- a convoy is a group of vehicles or ships travelling together, often with protection; it is not one vehicle
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “An aid convoy reached the village before nightfall.” (Đoàn xe cứu trợ đến ngôi làng trước khi trời tối.)
