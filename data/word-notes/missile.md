### Điểm cần nhớ
- guided missile; missile launch; ballistic missile
### Dễ nhầm
- a missile is a weapon designed to travel through the air and hit a target; a rocket may be a vehicle or propulsion system
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The missile was intercepted before reaching its target.” (Tên lửa bị đánh chặn trước khi tới mục tiêu.)
