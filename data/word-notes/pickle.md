### Điểm cần nhớ
- pickled cucumber; pickle a vegetable; in a pickle
### Dễ nhầm
- pickle can mean a cucumber preserved in vinegar or a preserved food generally; in a pickle means in a difficult situation
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She added a pickle to the sandwich.” (Cô thêm dưa chuột muối vào bánh mì.)
