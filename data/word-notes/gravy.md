### Điểm cần nhớ
- make gravy; gravy boat; gravy → sauce from meat juices
### Dễ nhầm
- gravy is a sauce made from meat juices and other ingredients; it is usually uncountable, though types can be called gravies
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She poured gravy over the roast potatoes.” (Cô rưới nước sốt thịt lên khoai tây nướng.)
