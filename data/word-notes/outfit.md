### Điểm cần nhớ
- wear an outfit; outfit somebody with equipment; outfit → clothing set
### Dễ nhầm
- an outfit is a set of clothes worn together or a group equipped for a purpose; costume is clothing for a role or event
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He chose a smart outfit for the interview.” (Anh chọn bộ trang phục lịch sự cho buổi phỏng vấn.)
