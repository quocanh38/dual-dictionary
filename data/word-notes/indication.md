### Điểm cần nhớ
- an indication of; give no indication; indication that something is happening
### Dễ nhầm
- indication is a sign or piece of information; indicate is the verb
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The dark clouds were an indication that the storm was close.” (Mây đen là dấu hiệu cho thấy cơn bão đang đến gần.)
