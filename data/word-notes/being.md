### Điểm cần nhớ
- being careful; being repaired; a human being; for the time being
### Dễ nhầm
- being is the -ing form of be; in passive forms it can be followed by a past participle
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The bridge is being repaired, so traffic is slow.” (Cây cầu đang được sửa nên giao thông chậm.)
