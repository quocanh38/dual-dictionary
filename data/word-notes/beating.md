### Điểm cần nhớ
- heartbeat; take a beating; beating a record; beat → beating
### Dễ nhầm
- beating can mean striking repeatedly, a pulse or a defeat; the phrase take a beating can be figurative
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The old engine kept beating steadily as the boat crossed the lake.” (Động cơ cũ vẫn nổ đều khi thuyền băng qua hồ.)
