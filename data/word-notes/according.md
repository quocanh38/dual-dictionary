### Điểm cần nhớ
- according to the report/person; according as (formal)
### Dễ nhầm
- according to introduces a source or standard; do not use it to state your own opinion without attribution
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “According to the timetable, the next train leaves at 6:20.” (Theo lịch, chuyến tàu tiếp theo rời ga lúc 6 giờ 20.)
