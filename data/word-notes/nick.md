### Điểm cần nhớ
- Nick as a given name; nick somebody’s idea; in the nick of time
### Dễ nhầm
- nick can be a name, informal British verb for steal, or part of fixed expressions; context is essential
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We reached the platform in the nick of time.” (Chúng tôi đến sân ga vừa kịp lúc.)
