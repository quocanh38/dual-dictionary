### Điểm cần nhớ
- video game console; console somebody; console → comfort
### Dễ nhầm
- a console is an electronic control unit or a verb meaning comfort someone; soothe is a close synonym for the verb
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She tried to console her friend after the loss.” (Cô cố an ủi bạn sau mất mát.)
