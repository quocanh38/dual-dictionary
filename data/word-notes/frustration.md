### Điểm cần nhớ
- express frustration; frustration with a system; frustration → annoyed helplessness
### Dễ nhầm
- frustration is the feeling of annoyance caused by being unable to achieve something; it is usually uncountable
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He sighed in frustration at the broken printer.” (Anh thở dài bực bội vì chiếc máy in bị hỏng.)
