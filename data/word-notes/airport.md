### Điểm cần nhớ
- at/to the airport; airport terminal/security; airport bus
### Dễ nhầm
- airport is the place where aircraft arrive/depart; airfield may refer to a smaller landing area
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We arrived at the airport two hours before departure.” (Chúng tôi đến sân bay hai tiếng trước giờ khởi hành.)
