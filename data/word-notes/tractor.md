### Điểm cần nhớ
- farm tractor; tractor driver; tractor trailer
### Dễ nhầm
- a tractor is a powerful vehicle used to pull farm equipment; in North America, tractor can also refer to the front unit of a large truck
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The farmer drove a tractor across the field.” (Người nông dân lái máy kéo qua cánh đồng.)
