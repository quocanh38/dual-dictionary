### Điểm cần nhớ
- blocking a doorway; theatrical blocking; block → blocking
### Dễ nhầm
- blocking means preventing movement or arranging performers’ positions on stage; obstructing emphasizes getting in the way
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The parked van was blocking the entrance.” (Chiếc xe tải đỗ chắn lối vào.)
