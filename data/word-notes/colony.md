### Điểm cần nhớ
- ant colony; British colony; colony → settlement/group
### Dễ nhầm
- a colony is a group of people or animals living together, or a territory controlled by another country; settlement is a place where people establish a community
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The ants built a colony under the stone.” (Đàn kiến xây tổ dưới tảng đá.)
