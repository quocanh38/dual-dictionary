### Điểm cần nhớ
- easily distracted; distracted by noise; distract → unable to focus
### Dễ nhầm
- distracted describes someone whose attention has been pulled away; distracting describes the thing that causes it
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He was distracted by messages on his phone.” (Anh bị những tin nhắn trên điện thoại làm phân tâm.)
