### Điểm cần nhớ
- tearing paper; tearing pain; tearing up
### Dễ nhầm
- tearing is the -ing form of tear; tear can mean rip something or produce tears from the eyes, with different pronunciation
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He was tearing the paper into small pieces.” (Anh xé tờ giấy thành những mảnh nhỏ.)
