### Điểm cần nhớ
- expanding business; expanding universe; expand → growing
### Dễ nhầm
- expanding is the -ing form of expand and means growing in size, number or scope; expanding a business is an active process
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The company is expanding into new markets.” (Công ty đang mở rộng sang các thị trường mới.)
