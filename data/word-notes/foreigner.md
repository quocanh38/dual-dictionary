### Điểm cần nhớ
- foreign visitor; treat someone as a foreigner; foreigner → person from another country
### Dễ nhầm
- foreigner means a person from a different country, but it can sound distancing or offensive; a person from [country] is often more neutral
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The guide welcomed visitors from abroad.” (Hướng dẫn viên chào đón những vị khách từ nước ngoài.)
