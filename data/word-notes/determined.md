### Điểm cần nhớ
- be determined to do something; determined effort; determine → determined
### Dễ nhầm
- determined means firmly decided or resolute; decisive describes someone who makes choices quickly
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He was determined to finish the project on time.” (Anh quyết tâm hoàn thành dự án đúng hạn.)
