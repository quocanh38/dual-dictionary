### Điểm cần nhớ
- associated with a group; associated risks; associate → associated
### Dễ nhầm
- associated means connected with someone or something; related often indicates a direct connection
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The symptoms are associated with a lack of sleep.” (Các triệu chứng có liên quan đến việc thiếu ngủ.)
