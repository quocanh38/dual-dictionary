### Điểm cần nhớ
- Scotch whisky; Scotch bonnet; scotch a plan
### Dễ nhầm
- Scotch can refer to whisky from Scotland or mean decisively stop something; Scottish is the general adjective for Scotland
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They served Scotch whisky after dinner.” (Họ phục vụ rượu whisky Scotland sau bữa tối.)
