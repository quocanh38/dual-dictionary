### Điểm cần nhớ
- lie to somebody; lied about something; lie → lied (tell an untruth)
### Dễ nhầm
- lied is the past/past participle of lie meaning tell an untruth; lie down has different forms: lay, lain
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He lied about where he had parked the car.” (Anh nói dối về chỗ đã đỗ xe.)
