### Điểm cần nhớ
- get into the bathtub; bathtub drain; bathtub → bath (UK)
### Dễ nhầm
- a bathtub is a container for bathing, especially in North American English; bath is common in British English
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The children played with toys in the bathtub.” (Bọn trẻ chơi đồ chơi trong bồn tắm.)
