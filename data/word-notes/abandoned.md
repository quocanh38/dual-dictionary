### Điểm cần nhớ
- abandoned building/vehicle; abandoned a plan; abandon → abandoned
### Dễ nhầm
- abandoned can mean left behind or given up; deserted often implies leaving a person/place without care
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The old station was abandoned after the new line opened.” (Nhà ga cũ bị bỏ hoang sau khi tuyến mới mở.)
