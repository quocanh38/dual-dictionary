### Điểm cần nhớ
- speed hump; hump of a camel; hump → raised rounded part
### Dễ nhầm
- a hump is a rounded raised part on a surface or an animal's back; a speed hump is a raised road feature that slows vehicles
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The camel stored fat in its hump.” (Con lạc đà dự trữ mỡ trong bướu.)
