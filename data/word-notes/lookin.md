### Điểm cần nhớ
- lookin’ good; keep lookin’; lookin’ → informal spelling of looking
### Dễ nhầm
- lookin’ is an informal spelling that represents the dropped final g in looking; use looking in standard writing
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- ““You’re lookin’ great,” he said.” (“Trông bạn tuyệt lắm,” anh nói.)
