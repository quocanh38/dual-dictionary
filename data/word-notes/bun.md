### Điểm cần nhớ
- hot cross bun; hair in a bun; bun → small bread roll
### Dễ nhầm
- a bun is a small round bread roll or a hairstyle in which hair is gathered behind the head; roll is a general bread term
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He bought a cinnamon bun for breakfast.” (Anh mua một chiếc bánh cuộn quế cho bữa sáng.)
