### Điểm cần nhớ
- accepting an offer; accepting of differences; accept → accepting
### Dễ nhầm
- accepting means agreeing to receive something or being willing to tolerate differences; receiving is more neutral
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She was accepting of different viewpoints.” (Cô sẵn lòng chấp nhận những quan điểm khác nhau.)
