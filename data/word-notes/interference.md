### Điểm cần nhớ
- interference in affairs; radio interference; interference → unwanted involvement or disruption
### Dễ nhầm
- interference can mean unwanted involvement in another person's affairs or disruption to a signal; the phrase interference with is also common
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The radio signal was weak because of interference.” (Tín hiệu radio yếu do nhiễu.)
