### Điểm cần nhớ
- have drunk; drunk driver; get drunk; drink → drank → drunk
### Dễ nhầm
- drunk is the past participle of drink and also an adjective; drank is the simple past
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She had drunk some water before the race began.” (Cô đã uống chút nước trước khi cuộc đua bắt đầu.)
