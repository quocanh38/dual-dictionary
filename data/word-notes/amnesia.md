### Điểm cần nhớ
- temporary amnesia; amnesia after an injury; amnesia → memory loss
### Dễ nhầm
- amnesia is loss of memory, often due to illness or injury; forgetfulness is milder and common
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The patient experienced amnesia after the accident.” (Bệnh nhân bị mất trí nhớ sau tai nạn.)
