### Điểm cần nhớ
- sit on a bench; bench press; bench somebody (sports)
### Dễ nhầm
- bench can be a seat, a judge’s position or a sports substitute area; bench as a verb can mean leave a player out
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They rested on a bench beside the river.” (Họ nghỉ trên ghế dài bên bờ sông.)
