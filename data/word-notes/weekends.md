### Điểm cần nhớ
- at weekends; on weekends; work weekends
### Dễ nhầm
- weekends is the plural of weekend or a verb form; British English often uses at weekends, while US English often uses on weekends
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The museum is busiest on weekends.” (Bảo tàng đông khách nhất vào cuối tuần.)
