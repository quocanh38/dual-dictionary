### Điểm cần nhớ
- bubonic plague; plague of insects; plague somebody with questions
### Dễ nhầm
- plague is a serious infectious disease or a large number of troublesome things; as a verb it means cause persistent trouble
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Mosquitoes plagued the village all summer.” (Muỗi quấy nhiễu ngôi làng suốt mùa hè.)
