### Điểm cần nhớ
- shaken by the news; shaken up; shaken hands
### Dễ nhầm
- shaken is the past participle of shake; it can describe emotional distress or the action of mixing something by shaking
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She was shaken by the sudden announcement.” (Cô bàng hoàng trước thông báo bất ngờ.)
