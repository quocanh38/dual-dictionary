### Điểm cần nhớ
- resolved the issue; resolved to act; conflict resolved
### Dễ nhầm
- resolved is the past form or participle of resolve; resolve a problem means settle it, while resolve to do something means decide firmly
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The team resolved the issue before launch.” (Nhóm giải quyết vấn đề trước khi ra mắt.)
