### Điểm cần nhớ
- political corruption; fight corruption; corruption → dishonest conduct
### Dễ nhầm
- corruption is dishonest or illegal behaviour, especially by people in authority; it is a noun, while corrupt is the adjective or verb
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The new law aims to reduce corruption.” (Luật mới nhằm giảm tham nhũng.)
