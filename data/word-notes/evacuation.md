### Điểm cần nhớ
- order an evacuation; evacuation route; evacuate → organised removal
### Dễ nhầm
- an evacuation is the process of moving people from a dangerous place to safety; evacuation route names the path to leave by
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The school practised an emergency evacuation.” (Trường học diễn tập sơ tán khẩn cấp.)
