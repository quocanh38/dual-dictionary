### Điểm cần nhớ
- bring disgrace on; fall into disgrace; disgrace → loss of respect
### Dễ nhầm
- disgrace is the loss of respect caused by bad behaviour; as a verb, disgrace someone means make them lose respect
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The scandal brought disgrace on the organisation.” (Vụ bê bối khiến tổ chức mất danh dự.)
