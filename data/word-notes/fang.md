### Điểm cần nhớ
- snake's fangs; fang marks; fang → long sharp tooth
### Dễ nhầm
- a fang is one of the long sharp teeth of an animal such as a snake or vampire in stories; it is usually used in the plural
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The snake showed its fangs when threatened.” (Con rắn nhe nanh khi bị đe dọa.)
