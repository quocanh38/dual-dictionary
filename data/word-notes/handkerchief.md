### Điểm cần nhớ
- cloth handkerchief; carry a handkerchief; handkerchief → small cloth for wiping
### Dễ nhầm
- a handkerchief is a small square of cloth used for wiping the nose or face; plural handkerchiefs is the most common form
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He folded the handkerchief and put it away.” (Anh gấp chiếc khăn tay rồi cất đi.)
