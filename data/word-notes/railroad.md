### Điểm cần nhớ
- railroad station; railroad company; railroad somebody into a decision
### Dễ nhầm
- railroad is the usual US word for railway; as a verb, railroad can mean force someone through a decision unfairly
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The railroad connects the town to the coast.” (Đường sắt nối thị trấn với bờ biển.)
