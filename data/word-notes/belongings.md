### Điểm cần nhớ
- personal belongings; pack your belongings; belonging → belongings
### Dễ nhầm
- belongings are the things someone owns and carries; possessions is a more formal synonym
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Please take your belongings with you when you leave.” (Vui lòng mang theo đồ đạc cá nhân khi rời đi.)
