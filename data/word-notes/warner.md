### Điểm cần nhớ
- a warner; warning signal; Warner as a surname
### Dễ nhầm
- warner can mean a person or thing that warns, though it is uncommon as a standalone noun; Warner is also a family name
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The warning served as a warner of the danger ahead.” (Lời cảnh báo báo hiệu nguy hiểm phía trước.)
