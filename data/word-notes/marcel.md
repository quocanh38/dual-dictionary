### Điểm cần nhớ
- marcel wave; have your hair marcelled; Marcel as a given name
### Dễ nhầm
- marcel can mean a hairstyle with repeated waves or the verb for styling hair that way; capitalized Marcel is a masculine given name
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She wore her hair in a marcel wave.” (Cô để tóc uốn sóng kiểu marcel.)
