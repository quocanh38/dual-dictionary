### Điểm cần nhớ
- get pissed; pissed off; piss → vulgar informal
### Dễ nhầm
- pissed can mean drunk in British English or angry in “pissed off” in North American English; it is vulgar and informal
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He was pissed off when the meeting was cancelled.” (Anh bực mình khi cuộc họp bị hủy.)
