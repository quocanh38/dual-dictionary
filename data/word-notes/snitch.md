### Điểm cần nhớ
- snitch on somebody; a snitch; snitch to the teacher
### Dễ nhầm
- snitch is informal for inform on someone or the person who does so; it often carries a disapproving tone
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He was afraid his friends would call him a snitch.” (Anh sợ bạn bè sẽ gọi mình là kẻ mách lẻo.)
