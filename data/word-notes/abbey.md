### Điểm cần nhớ
- Westminster Abbey; abbey church; abbey → monastery
### Dễ nhầm
- an abbey is a religious building or community led by an abbot or abbess; monastery is a general term for a community of monks
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The old abbey attracts visitors from around the world.” (Tu viện cổ thu hút du khách từ khắp nơi.)
