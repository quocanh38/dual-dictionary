### Điểm cần nhớ
- feel confused; confused about something; confuse somebody with something
### Dễ nhầm
- confused describes a person who cannot understand clearly; confusing describes something that causes this feeling
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “I was confused by the map’s unfamiliar symbols.” (Tôi bối rối trước những ký hiệu lạ trên bản đồ.)
