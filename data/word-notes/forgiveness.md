### Điểm cần nhớ
- ask for forgiveness; seek forgiveness; forgive → forgiveness
### Dễ nhầm
- forgiveness is choosing to stop feeling anger toward someone; pardon can be an official act or a polite request to repeat something
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He asked his friend for forgiveness.” (Anh xin bạn tha thứ.)
