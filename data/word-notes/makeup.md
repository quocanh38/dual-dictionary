### Điểm cần nhớ
- wear makeup; makeup artist; makeup → composition (different sense)
### Dễ nhầm
- makeup is cosmetics worn on the face or the way something is composed; make-up is also commonly hyphenated in British English
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She used very little makeup for the interview.” (Cô trang điểm rất nhẹ cho buổi phỏng vấn.)
