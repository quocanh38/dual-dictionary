### Điểm cần nhớ
- convict somebody of a crime; convicted prisoner; convict → convicted person
### Dễ nhầm
- convict means officially find someone guilty, or a person found guilty; suspect has not been proven guilty
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The jury convicted him of fraud.” (Bồi thẩm đoàn kết tội anh ta gian lận.)
