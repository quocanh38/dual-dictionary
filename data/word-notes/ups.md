### Điểm cần nhớ
- ups and downs; ups the price; give someone the ups
### Dễ nhầm
- ups is the plural of up or a verb form; ups and downs means both good and bad periods, while UPS is a delivery company
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Every relationship has its ups and downs.” (Mối quan hệ nào cũng có lúc thăng trầm.)
