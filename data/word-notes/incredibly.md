### Điểm cần nhớ
- incredibly useful; incredibly difficult; incredible → incredibly
### Dễ nhầm
- incredibly intensifies an adjective or means in a way that is hard to believe; extremely is more neutral
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The room was incredibly quiet after the announcement.” (Căn phòng yên lặng đến lạ sau thông báo.)
