### Điểm cần nhớ
- car parker; Parker (surname); park → parker
### Dễ nhầm
- parker can refer to someone who parks a vehicle, though the more usual job title is parking attendant; Parker is also a surname
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The parking attendant helped the parker find a space.” (Nhân viên trông xe giúp người lái tìm chỗ đỗ.)
