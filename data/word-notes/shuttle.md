### Điểm cần nhớ
- space shuttle; shuttle bus; shuttle between two offices
### Dễ nhầm
- a shuttle is a vehicle that travels regularly between places; as a verb it means travel back and forth
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “A shuttle bus runs between the airport and the hotel.” (Xe buýt trung chuyển chạy giữa sân bay và khách sạn.)
