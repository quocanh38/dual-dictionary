### Điểm cần nhớ
- fall overboard; throw something overboard; go overboard
### Dễ nhầm
- overboard means over the side of a boat; go overboard figuratively means do or spend too much
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He fell overboard but was rescued quickly.” (Anh rơi khỏi thuyền nhưng nhanh chóng được cứu.)
