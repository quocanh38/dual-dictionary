### Điểm cần nhớ
- drilling a hole; drilling exercise; drill → drilling
### Dễ nhầm
- drilling is the -ing form of drill and can mean making a hole, practising repeatedly, or extracting oil or gas
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Workers were drilling through the concrete.” (Công nhân đang khoan xuyên qua bê tông.)
