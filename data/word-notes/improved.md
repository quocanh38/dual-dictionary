### Điểm cần nhớ
- improved service; improved after treatment; improve → became or made better
### Dễ nhầm
- improved is the past form of improve and can describe a result that is better; improve on means make something better than a previous version
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The new route has improved travel times.” (Tuyến đường mới giúp rút ngắn thời gian di chuyển.)
