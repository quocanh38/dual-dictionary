### Điểm cần nhớ
- dreadful weather; dreadful mistake; dreadful → very bad
### Dễ nhầm
- dreadful means very bad or unpleasant and is especially common in British English; it is stronger than simply poor
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We had dreadful weather during the trip.” (Thời tiết trong chuyến đi thật tồi tệ.)
