### Điểm cần nhớ
- stay grounded; grounded aircraft; ground → realistic or connected to ground
### Dễ nhầm
- grounded can mean sensible and realistic, connected to the ground electrically, or prevented from flying; context distinguishes the senses
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The storm grounded flights across the region.” (Cơn bão khiến các chuyến bay trong khu vực phải nằm sân.)
