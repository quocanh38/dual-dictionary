### Điểm cần nhớ
- rotten fruit; rotten to the core; rotten luck
### Dễ nhầm
- rotten means decayed, morally bad or very unpleasant informally; spoiled is common for food that has gone bad
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The apples were rotten and had to be thrown away.” (Những quả táo bị thối và phải bỏ đi.)
