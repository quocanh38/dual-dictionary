### Điểm cần nhớ
- ashes of a fire; a person’s ashes; rise from the ashes
### Dễ nhầm
- ashes is plural of ash and is commonly used for the remains after burning; ash can be the substance in general
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They scattered the ashes beneath the oak tree.” (Họ rải tro dưới gốc cây sồi.)
