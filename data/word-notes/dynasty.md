### Điểm cần nhớ
- royal dynasty; dynasty of rulers; dynasty → succession of same-family leaders
### Dễ nhầm
- a dynasty is a series of rulers from the same family; in sports, it can mean a team that dominates for many years
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The dynasty ruled the region for centuries.” (Triều đại đó cai trị khu vực trong nhiều thế kỷ.)
