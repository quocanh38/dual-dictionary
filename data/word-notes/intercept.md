### Điểm cần nhớ
- intercept a message; intercept a pass; intercept → stop before destination
### Dễ nhầm
- to intercept means stop or catch someone or something before it reaches its destination; it is often used for messages, passes or vehicles
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The defender intercepted the pass near the goal.” (Hậu vệ chặn đường chuyền gần khung thành.)
