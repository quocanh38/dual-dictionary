### Điểm cần nhớ
- induced labour; induced a response; drug-induced
### Dễ nhầm
- induced is the past form of induce, meaning cause something to happen; in medicine it can refer to bringing on labour or a condition
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The treatment induced a mild reaction.” (Phương pháp điều trị gây ra phản ứng nhẹ.)
