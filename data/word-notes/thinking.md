### Điểm cần nhớ
- critical/creative thinking; be thinking about; thinking cap
### Dễ nhầm
- thinking can be the -ing form of think or a noun for the process of thought; think → thought → thought
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She is thinking about applying for the research position.” (Cô đang cân nhắc nộp đơn vào vị trí nghiên cứu.)
