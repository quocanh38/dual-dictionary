### Điểm cần nhớ
- dragging a suitcase; dragging on; drag → pull along
### Dễ nhầm
- dragging is the -ing form of drag, meaning pull something along a surface; an event that is dragging on is taking too long
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He was dragging the suitcase over the cobblestones.” (Anh kéo chiếc vali trên con đường lát đá.)
