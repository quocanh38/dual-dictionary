### Điểm cần nhớ
- magnet attracts metal; magnetic field; refrigerator magnet
### Dễ nhầm
- a magnet attracts certain metals and has north and south poles; magnetic means produced by or like a magnet
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The magnet held the note to the fridge.” (Nam châm giữ tờ giấy trên tủ lạnh.)
