### Điểm cần nhớ
- divided opinion; divided into groups; divide → separated/shared
### Dễ nhầm
- divided describes something separated into parts or people who disagree; divide can also mean share something among people
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The committee was divided on the proposal.” (Ủy ban có ý kiến chia rẽ về đề xuất.)
