### Điểm cần nhớ
- assault rifle; rifle through papers; rifleman
### Dễ nhầm
- a rifle is a long gun with a grooved barrel; as a verb, rifle through means search quickly and carelessly
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The officer carried a rifle during the patrol.” (Viên cảnh sát mang theo súng trường trong lúc tuần tra.)
