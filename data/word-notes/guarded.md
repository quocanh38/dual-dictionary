### Điểm cần nhớ
- guarded response; guarded optimism; guarded → cautious and reserved
### Dễ nhầm
- guarded describes someone careful not to reveal feelings or information; guarded optimism means hope expressed with caution
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The minister gave a guarded response to the proposal.” (Bộ trưởng đưa ra phản hồi dè dặt về đề xuất.)
