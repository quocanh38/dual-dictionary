### Điểm cần nhớ
- vest in a company; vest someone with authority; wear a vest
### Dễ nhầm
- vest can mean a sleeveless undergarment or waistcoat in some varieties; as a verb it means give a right or put authority in someone
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The board vested her with full authority.” (Hội đồng trao cho cô toàn quyền.)
