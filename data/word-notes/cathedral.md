### Điểm cần nhớ
- Gothic cathedral; cathedral city; cathedral → bishop’s church
### Dễ nhầm
- a cathedral is the main church of a diocese, where a bishop’s seat is located; church is the general term
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The cathedral took centuries to complete.” (Nhà thờ chính tòa mất hàng thế kỷ mới hoàn thành.)
