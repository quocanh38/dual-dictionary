### Điểm cần nhớ
- stabbing pain; stabbing someone; stabbing incident
### Dễ nhầm
- stabbing is the -ing form of stab; a stabbing pain feels sudden and sharp, while a stabbing incident involves a knife attack
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She felt a stabbing pain in her side.” (Cô cảm thấy đau nhói ở bên sườn.)
