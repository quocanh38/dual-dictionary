### Điểm cần nhớ
- humiliating defeat; humiliating experience; humiliate → causing shame
### Dễ nhầm
- humiliating describes an event that makes someone feel ashamed; humiliated describes the person who feels that way
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Losing by such a large margin was humiliating.” (Thua với cách biệt lớn như vậy thật đáng xấu hổ.)
