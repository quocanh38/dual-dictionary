### Điểm cần nhớ
- Don + name is a Spanish title; don a coat; don’t = do not
### Dễ nhầm
- don as a verb means put on clothing and is formal; don’t is a contraction and needs an apostrophe
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He donned a rain jacket before stepping outside.” (Anh khoác áo mưa trước khi bước ra ngoài.)
