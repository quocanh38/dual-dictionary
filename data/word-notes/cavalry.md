### Điểm cần nhớ
- cavalry charge; cavalry officer; cavalry → mounted soldiers
### Dễ nhầm
- cavalry refers to soldiers who fight on horseback or in armoured vehicles; infantry are soldiers who fight on foot
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The cavalry advanced across the open field.” (Kỵ binh tiến qua cánh đồng trống.)
