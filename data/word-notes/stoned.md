### Điểm cần nhớ
- get stoned; stoned to death; stoned fruit
### Dễ nhầm
- stoned is the past form of stone; informal get stoned means become affected by drugs, while stoned fruit has its stone removed
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The police found him stoned and disoriented.” (Cảnh sát tìm thấy anh trong tình trạng phê thuốc và mất phương hướng.)
