### Điểm cần nhớ
- heavenly music; heavenly taste; heavenly → connected with heaven or wonderful
### Dễ nhầm
- heavenly can mean connected with heaven or, informally, extremely pleasant; it is often used for food, music or scenery
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The dessert tasted heavenly.” (Món tráng miệng ngon tuyệt.)
