### Điểm cần nhớ
- jaw bone; lower jaw; jaw → bone structure of the mouth
### Dễ nhầm
- the jaw is the bone structure that holds the teeth; jaw can also mean talk at length in informal expressions
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He clenched his jaw when he heard the news.” (Anh nghiến chặt hàm khi nghe tin.)
