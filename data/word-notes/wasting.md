### Điểm cần nhớ
- wasting time; wasting disease; waste → wasting
### Dễ nhầm
- wasting means using something carelessly or becoming weak and thin; deteriorating describes becoming worse more generally
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Stop wasting water while you brush your teeth.” (Đừng lãng phí nước khi đánh răng.)
