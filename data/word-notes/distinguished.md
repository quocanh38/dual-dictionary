### Điểm cần nhớ
- distinguished career; distinguished guest; distinguished → successful and respected
### Dễ nhầm
- distinguished often means successful and admired; it can also describe a dignified appearance, not merely someone who is different
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She had a distinguished career in medicine.” (Bà có sự nghiệp y khoa đáng kính nể.)
