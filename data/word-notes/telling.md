### Điểm cần nhớ
- tell the truth; telling somebody to do; a telling sign; tell → telling
### Dễ nhầm
- telling can be the -ing form of tell or an adjective meaning revealing; tell → told → told
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The quiet room was a telling sign that the event had ended.” (Căn phòng yên ắng là dấu hiệu rõ cho thấy sự kiện đã kết thúc.)
