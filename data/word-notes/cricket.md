### Điểm cần nhớ
- play cricket; cricket match; cricket → bat-and-ball sport
### Dễ nhầm
- cricket is a team sport played with a bat and ball, usually by teams of eleven; the same word also names a small chirping insect
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They watched a cricket match on the lawn.” (Họ xem một trận cricket trên bãi cỏ.)
