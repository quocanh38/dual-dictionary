### Điểm cần nhớ
- old hulk; hulk of a ship; hulk → large damaged structure
### Dễ nhầm
- a hulk is the main remains of an old ship, vehicle or large structure that is no longer used; it often suggests something huge and worn out
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The hulk of the ship remained in the harbour.” (Phần xác tàu lớn vẫn nằm trong bến cảng.)
