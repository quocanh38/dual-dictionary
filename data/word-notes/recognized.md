### Điểm cần nhớ
- internationally recognized; recognized a voice; recognize → recognized
### Dễ nhầm
- recognized is the US spelling of recognised and is the past form of recognize; it means identified or officially accepted
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The artist was recognized for her contribution to music.” (Nữ nghệ sĩ được ghi nhận vì đóng góp cho âm nhạc.)
