### Điểm cần nhớ
- balcony seats; balcony garden; balcony → raised platform
### Dễ nhầm
- a balcony is a platform projecting from the outside wall of a building; terrace is usually larger and may be at ground level
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They had breakfast on the hotel balcony.” (Họ ăn sáng ngoài ban công khách sạn.)
