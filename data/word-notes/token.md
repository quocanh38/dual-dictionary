### Điểm cần nhớ
- a token of appreciation; bus token; token gesture
### Dễ nhầm
- a token is a small object representing something or an action done as a sign; token can imply that the gesture is symbolic rather than substantial
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They gave her flowers as a token of thanks.” (Họ tặng hoa để bày tỏ lòng cảm ơn.)
