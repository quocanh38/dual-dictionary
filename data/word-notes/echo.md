### Điểm cần nhớ
- echo through a hall; echo someone's words; echo → reflected sound/repeat
### Dễ nhầm
- an echo is a sound heard again after reflecting from a surface; as a verb, echo can mean repeat words or ideas
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Her voice echoed through the empty hall.” (Giọng cô vang vọng khắp sảnh trống.)
