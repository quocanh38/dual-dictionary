### Điểm cần nhớ
- get going; getting better; get somebody to do something; get → got → got/gotten
### Dễ nhầm
- getting is the -ing form of get; US English often uses gotten as the past participle, while British English usually uses got
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The weather is getting warmer each day.” (Thời tiết đang ấm lên mỗi ngày.)
