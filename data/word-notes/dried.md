### Điểm cần nhớ
- dried fruit; dried flowers; dry → dried
### Dễ nhầm
- dried describes something from which liquid has been removed, often to preserve it; dry can also describe something that is not wet
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We added dried herbs to the soup.” (Chúng tôi thêm thảo mộc khô vào súp.)
