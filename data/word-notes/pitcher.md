### Điểm cần nhớ
- water pitcher; baseball pitcher; pitcher of lemonade
### Dễ nhầm
- pitcher means a container for pouring liquid in US English or a player who throws the ball in baseball; British English commonly uses jug for the container
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She filled the pitcher with iced tea.” (Cô rót trà đá đầy bình.)
