### Điểm cần nhớ
- jasmine flowers; jasmine tea; jasmine → fragrant flowering plant
### Dễ nhầm
- jasmine is a plant with fragrant white or yellow flowers; the name is usually uncountable when referring to the plant generally
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The jasmine filled the garden with fragrance.” (Hoa nhài tỏa hương khắp khu vườn.)
