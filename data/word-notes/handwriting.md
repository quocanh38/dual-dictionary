### Điểm cần nhớ
- read someone's handwriting; handwriting style; handwriting → writing done by hand
### Dễ nhầm
- handwriting is the style or act of writing by hand and is usually uncountable; say someone's handwriting, not a handwriting
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Her handwriting was neat and easy to read.” (Chữ viết tay của cô gọn gàng và dễ đọc.)
