### Điểm cần nhớ
- a cue to begin; take your cue from; cue → signal
### Dễ nhầm
- a cue is a signal that tells someone when to act; take your cue from someone means follow their example or response
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The music was her cue to walk onstage.” (Âm nhạc là tín hiệu để cô bước lên sân khấu.)
