### Điểm cần nhớ
- hack at wood; hack into a computer; hack → cut roughly or gain unauthorised access
### Dễ nhầm
- hack can mean cut roughly, gain unauthorised access to a computer system, or do a task in a rough way; context changes the meaning
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The old branch was too thick to hack through.” (Cành cây cũ quá dày nên khó chặt đứt.)
