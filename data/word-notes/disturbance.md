### Điểm cần nhớ
- cause a disturbance; sleep disturbance; disturbance → interruption/disorder
### Dễ nhầm
- a disturbance is an interruption or a situation in which normal order is disrupted; it can also mean a change in a physical system
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The neighbours reported a disturbance after midnight.” (Hàng xóm báo có vụ gây rối sau nửa đêm.)
