### Điểm cần nhớ
- whispering softly; whispering campaign; whisper → whispering
### Dễ nhầm
- whispering means speaking very quietly without using the full voice; murmuring is a low, indistinct way of speaking
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The students were whispering during the film.” (Các học sinh thì thầm trong lúc xem phim.)
