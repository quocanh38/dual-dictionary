### Điểm cần nhớ
- bathe a baby; bathe in sunlight; bathe → wash/swim
### Dễ nhầm
- bathe means wash someone or swim for pleasure, especially in British English; bathe in sunlight means be exposed to it
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She bathed the baby before bedtime.” (Cô tắm cho em bé trước giờ đi ngủ.)
