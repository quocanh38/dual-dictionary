### Điểm cần nhớ
- bounty hunter; rich bounty; bounty → reward/plenty
### Dễ nhầm
- bounty is a reward offered for finding someone or something, or a large amount of something; reward is the general term
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The government offered a bounty for information.” (Chính phủ treo thưởng cho thông tin.)
