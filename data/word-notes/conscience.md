### Điểm cần nhớ
- a clear conscience; prick somebody’s conscience; conscience-stricken
### Dễ nhầm
- conscience is the inner sense of right and wrong; consciousness means being awake and aware
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Her conscience would not let her keep the secret.” (Lương tâm không cho phép cô giữ bí mật.)
