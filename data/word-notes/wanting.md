### Điểm cần nhớ
- leave much to be desired; wanting something; want → wanting
### Dễ nhầm
- wanting can mean desiring something or being inadequate; lacking is a more direct word for not having enough
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The service was found wanting during the busy period.” (Dịch vụ bị đánh giá là chưa đạt trong thời gian cao điểm.)
