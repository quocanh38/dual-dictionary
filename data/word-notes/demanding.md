### Điểm cần nhớ
- demanding job; demanding customer; demanding → requiring much effort
### Dễ nhầm
- demanding describes work that needs considerable effort or a person who expects a lot; it does not automatically mean unreasonable
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Caring for the patients was physically demanding.” (Chăm sóc bệnh nhân đòi hỏi nhiều sức lực.)
