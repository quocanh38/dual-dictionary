### Điểm cần nhớ
- jealous of somebody; jealous that; jealous look
### Dễ nhầm
- jealous usually describes fear of losing affection or resentment of someone’s advantage; envious focuses on wanting what another has
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He was jealous of the attention his colleague received.” (Anh ghen tị với sự chú ý mà đồng nghiệp nhận được.)
