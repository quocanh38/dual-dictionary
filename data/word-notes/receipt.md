### Điểm cần nhớ
- keep a receipt; receipt for payment; acknowledge receipt
### Dễ nhầm
- a receipt is proof that money or goods were received; in British English, receipt is pronounced with a silent p
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Keep the receipt in case you need a refund.” (Giữ hóa đơn phòng khi bạn cần hoàn tiền.)
