### Điểm cần nhớ
- feel depressed; clinically depressed; depress → depressed
### Dễ nhầm
- depressed can mean very unhappy or affected by clinical depression; sad describes an emotion and does not imply a condition
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He felt depressed after losing his job.” (Anh ấy cảm thấy suy sụp sau khi mất việc.)
