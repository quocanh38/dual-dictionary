### Điểm cần nhớ
- feel guilt; guilt about something; guilty conscience
### Dễ nhầm
- guilt is the feeling of having done something wrong; shame is often concern about how one appears to others
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He felt guilt for not visiting his friend.” (Anh cảm thấy có lỗi vì đã không đến thăm bạn.)
