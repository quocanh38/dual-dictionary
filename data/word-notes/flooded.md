### Điểm cần nhớ
- flooded street; flooded with light; flood → covered with water
### Dễ nhầm
- flooded describes an area covered with water; figuratively, a room flooded with light is filled with it
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The flooded road was closed to traffic.” (Con đường ngập nước đã bị chặn xe.)
