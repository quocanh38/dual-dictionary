### Điểm cần nhớ
- gain admission; admission of guilt; admission fee
### Dễ nhầm
- admission can mean permission to enter, the act of accepting a fact or the fee to enter; entrance is the act or place of going in
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The museum charges an admission fee.” (Bảo tàng thu phí vào cửa.)
