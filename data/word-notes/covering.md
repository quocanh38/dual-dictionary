### Điểm cần nhớ
- covering letter; covering the cost; cover → covering
### Dễ nhầm
- covering can mean putting a layer over something, reporting a subject or including a cost; coverage is the extent of protection or reporting
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The insurance is covering the cost of repairs.” (Bảo hiểm sẽ chi trả chi phí sửa chữa.)
