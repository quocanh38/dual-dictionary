### Điểm cần nhớ
- developing country; developing situation; developing → growing/changing
### Dễ nhầm
- developing can describe something that is growing or changing; a developing country is an established term for a country with lower industrialisation and income levels
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The team is monitoring the developing situation.” (Nhóm đang theo dõi tình hình đang diễn biến.)
