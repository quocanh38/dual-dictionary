### Điểm cần nhớ
- earning money; earning potential; earn → earning
### Dễ nhầm
- earning is the -ing form of earn; earnings usually means the money someone receives from work or a company's profits
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She is earning enough to support herself.” (Cô ấy kiếm đủ tiền để tự trang trải.)
