### Điểm cần nhớ
- show hospitality; hospitality industry; hospitality → friendly welcome
### Dễ nhầm
- hospitality is friendly and generous treatment of guests and is uncountable; the hospitality industry includes hotels, restaurants and tourism
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We were grateful for their warm hospitality.” (Chúng tôi biết ơn sự hiếu khách nồng hậu của họ.)
