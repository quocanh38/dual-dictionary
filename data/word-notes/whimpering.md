### Điểm cần nhớ
- whimpering with fear; whimpering puppy; whimper → whimpering
### Dễ nhầm
- whimpering is making a series of low, weak sounds from fear or pain; whining is similar and often expresses complaint
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The frightened dog was whimpering under the table.” (Con chó sợ hãi rên ư ử dưới gầm bàn.)
