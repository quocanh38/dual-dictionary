### Điểm cần nhớ
- the Gulf; a gulf between opinions; gulf → partly enclosed sea or wide difference
### Dễ nhầm
- a gulf is a large area of sea partly surrounded by land; figuratively, a gulf is a very large difference between people or ideas
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “A wide gulf separated the two proposals.” (Một khoảng cách lớn ngăn cách hai đề xuất.)
