### Điểm cần nhớ
- give an injection; injection of funds; injection → act of putting liquid in
### Dễ nhầm
- an injection is medicine put into the body with a needle; figuratively, an injection of money or energy is a sudden addition
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The patient received an injection before the procedure.” (Bệnh nhân được tiêm trước thủ thuật.)
