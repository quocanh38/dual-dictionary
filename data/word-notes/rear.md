### Điểm cần nhớ
- rear door/entrance; at the rear of; rear a child
### Dễ nhầm
- rear as an adjective/noun is more formal for the back; raise a child is more common than rear a child
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The delivery entrance is at the rear of the building.” (Lối giao hàng nằm phía sau tòa nhà.)
