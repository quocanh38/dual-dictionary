### Điểm cần nhớ
- written confirmation; confirmation of a booking; confirm → confirmation
### Dễ nhầm
- confirmation is proof or a statement that something is definite; verification is the act of checking it is true
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We received confirmation of the reservation by email.” (Chúng tôi nhận được xác nhận đặt chỗ qua email.)
