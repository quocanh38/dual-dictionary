### Điểm cần nhớ
- suspended from school; suspended animation; suspend → suspended
### Dễ nhầm
- suspended means temporarily stopped or hanging from above; postponed usually means delayed to a later time
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The match was suspended because of heavy rain.” (Trận đấu bị tạm dừng vì mưa lớn.)
