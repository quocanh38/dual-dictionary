### Điểm cần nhớ
- clinical psychologist; educational psychologist; psychologist's report
### Dễ nhầm
- a psychologist studies or treats mental processes and behaviour; unlike a psychiatrist, a psychologist is not necessarily a medical doctor
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The psychologist helped him manage stress.” (Nhà tâm lý học giúp anh kiểm soát căng thẳng.)
