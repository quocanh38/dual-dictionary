### Điểm cần nhớ
- reinforced concrete; reinforced an argument; reinforced by evidence
### Dễ nhầm
- reinforced is the past form of reinforce, meaning strengthen or support with additional material or evidence
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The bridge was reinforced with steel beams.” (Cây cầu được gia cố bằng các dầm thép.)
