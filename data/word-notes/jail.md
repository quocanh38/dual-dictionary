### Điểm cần nhớ
- in jail; go to jail; jail somebody; jail sentence
### Dễ nhầm
- jail is common in US English; prison is more usual in British English for the institution, though both occur
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The court ordered him to spend six months in jail.” (Tòa tuyên anh ta sáu tháng tù.)
