### Điểm cần nhớ
- crooked streets; crooked smile; crooked deal; crooked → bent/dishonest
### Dễ nhầm
- crooked can mean bent or not straight, and informally can describe dishonest people or dealings; straight is the opposite in the physical sense
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The picture is crooked, so please straighten it.” (Bức tranh bị lệch, vui lòng chỉnh cho thẳng.)
