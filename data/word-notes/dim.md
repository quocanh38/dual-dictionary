### Điểm cần nhớ
- dim light; dim memory; dim → not bright/unclear
### Dễ nhầm
- dim means not bright or not clearly seen; figuratively, a dim memory is unclear, while dim can also mean not intelligent and may sound insulting
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “A dim light was visible under the door.” (Có ánh đèn mờ hắt ra dưới cánh cửa.)
