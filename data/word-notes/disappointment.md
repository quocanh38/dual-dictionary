### Điểm cần nhớ
- express disappointment; disappointment in; disappointment → feeling/result
### Dễ nhầm
- disappointment is the feeling of sadness when expectations are not met, or the person or thing that causes it; disappointing describes the cause
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The cancellation was a disappointment to the whole team.” (Việc hủy bỏ là điều khiến cả nhóm thất vọng.)
