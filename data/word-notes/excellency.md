### Điểm cần nhớ
- Your Excellency; His Excellency; excellency → title
### Dễ nhầm
- Excellency is a formal title used when addressing certain senior officials or diplomats; it is not a general synonym for excellence
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The ambassador was welcomed as Your Excellency.” (Đại sứ được chào đón bằng danh xưng “Ngài”.)
