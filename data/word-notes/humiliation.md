### Điểm cần nhớ
- public humiliation; suffer humiliation; humiliation → loss of dignity
### Dễ nhầm
- humiliation is the painful feeling or experience of being made to look foolish or lose dignity
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He could not hide his humiliation after the mistake.” (Anh không thể che giấu sự bẽ bàng sau sai lầm.)
