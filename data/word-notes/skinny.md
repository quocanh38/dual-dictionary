### Điểm cần nhớ
- skinny jeans; get the skinny on something; skinny → very thin
### Dễ nhầm
- skinny means very thin or, informally, the inside information; slim is usually more positive when describing a person
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The dog was skinny after being lost for a week.” (Con chó gầy đi sau khi bị lạc một tuần.)
