### Điểm cần nhớ
- inherited a house; inherited trait; inherit → received from someone
### Dễ nhầm
- inherited is the past form of inherit; an inherited trait is passed through a family, while inherited property is received from someone
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She inherited her patience from her mother.” (Cô thừa hưởng tính kiên nhẫn từ mẹ.)
