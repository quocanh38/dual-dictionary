### Điểm cần nhớ
- vegetarian diet; vegetarian meal; become a vegetarian
### Dễ nhầm
- a vegetarian does not eat meat or fish; some vegetarians eat eggs and dairy, while vegan excludes all animal products

- “The restaurant has several vegetarian dishes.” (Nhà hàng có một số món chay.)
