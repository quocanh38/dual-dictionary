### Điểm cần nhớ
- strictly prohibited; prohibited substance; prohibited from entering
### Dễ nhầm
- prohibited is the past form of prohibit, meaning officially forbid; prohibited from is followed by a noun or -ing form
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Photography is prohibited inside the gallery.” (Không được phép chụp ảnh bên trong phòng trưng bày.)
