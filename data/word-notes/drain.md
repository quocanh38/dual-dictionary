### Điểm cần nhớ
- drain water; drain your energy; drain → remove liquid/use up
### Dễ nhầm
- to drain means remove liquid from something or make a resource run low; a drain is also a pipe or channel carrying water away
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Drain the pasta before adding the sauce.” (Để ráo mì trước khi thêm nước sốt.)
