### Điểm cần nhớ
- abort a mission; abort a pregnancy; abortion
### Dễ nhầm
- abort means stop a process before it is complete; in medical contexts, use the term carefully and without stigma
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The pilot had to abort the landing because of strong winds.” (Phi công phải hủy hạ cánh vì gió mạnh.)
