### Điểm cần nhớ
- measured response; measured tone; measured in metres
### Dễ nhầm
- measured can mean calculated with a unit or calm and carefully controlled; it is also the past form of measure
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She gave a measured response to the criticism.” (Cô bình tĩnh đáp lại lời chỉ trích.)
