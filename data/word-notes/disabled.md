### Điểm cần nhớ
- disabled people; disabled access; disable → having an impairment
### Dễ nhầm
- disabled describes a person or facility affected by a disability; many style guides prefer “disabled people” or the person's specific condition over using “the disabled” as a group label
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The building has step-free access for disabled visitors.” (Tòa nhà có lối đi không bậc dành cho khách khuyết tật.)
