### Điểm cần nhớ
- bass guitar; bass voice; bass → low musical range/fish
### Dễ nhầm
- bass can mean a low musical range or several kinds of fish; the musical and fish senses have different pronunciations
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He plays bass in a jazz band.” (Anh chơi đàn bass trong ban nhạc jazz.)
