### Điểm cần nhớ
- vanished into thin air; vanished overnight; vanished without a trace
### Dễ nhầm
- vanished is the past form of vanish, meaning disappeared; vanish is intransitive and does not take a direct object
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The keys had vanished from the desk.” (Chìa khóa đã biến mất khỏi bàn.)
