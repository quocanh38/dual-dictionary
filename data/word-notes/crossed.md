### Điểm cần nhớ
- cross the road; fingers crossed; cross → crossed
### Dễ nhầm
- crossed can mean went from one side to another or became angry in British English; across is a preposition/adverb
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They crossed the bridge before sunset.” (Họ băng qua cây cầu trước khi mặt trời lặn.)
