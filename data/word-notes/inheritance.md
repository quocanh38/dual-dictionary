### Điểm cần nhớ
- inheritance tax; receive an inheritance; inheritance → property or quality passed down
### Dễ nhầm
- an inheritance is money or property received from someone who has died, or a quality passed down through generations
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He used his inheritance to pay off the mortgage.” (Anh dùng tài sản thừa kế để trả hết khoản vay mua nhà.)
