### Điểm cần nhớ
- overlapping schedules; overlapping circles; overlapping interests
### Dễ nhầm
- overlapping is the -ing form of overlap or an adjective meaning partly covering the same area or time
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The two projects have overlapping deadlines.” (Hai dự án có thời hạn trùng lặp.)
