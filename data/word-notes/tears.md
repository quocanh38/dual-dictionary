### Điểm cần nhớ
- in tears; tears of joy; tear up a paper; tear → tears
### Dễ nhầm
- tears (crying) and tears (rips) are spelled alike but pronounced differently; the verb tear → tore → torn
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She was in tears when she heard the good news.” (Cô rơi nước mắt khi nghe tin vui.)
