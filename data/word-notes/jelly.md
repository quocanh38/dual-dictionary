### Điểm cần nhớ
- jelly and toast; jelly dessert; jelly → fruit spread (US) or gel dessert (UK)
### Dễ nhầm
- in British English, jelly is a gel dessert; in North American English, jelly is a fruit spread, while the gel dessert is usually called Jell-O or gelatin
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “We had strawberry jelly for dessert.” (Chúng tôi ăn thạch dâu làm món tráng miệng.)
