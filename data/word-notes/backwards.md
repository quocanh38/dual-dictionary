### Điểm cần nhớ
- walk backwards; backwards and forwards; backwards compatible
### Dễ nhầm
- backwards is common in British English; backward is more common in American English and also an adjective
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The child walked backwards toward the sofa.” (Đứa trẻ đi lùi về phía ghế sofa.)
