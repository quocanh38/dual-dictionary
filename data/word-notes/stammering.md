### Điểm cần nhớ
- stammering reply; stammering with fear; stammer
### Dễ nhầm
- stammering is speaking with involuntary pauses or repeated sounds; stuttering is a common synonym, especially in US English
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “He gave a stammering reply to the question.” (Anh lắp bắp trả lời câu hỏi.)
