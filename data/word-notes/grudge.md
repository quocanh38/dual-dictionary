### Điểm cần nhớ
- hold a grudge against; bear no grudge; grudge → lasting resentment
### Dễ nhầm
- a grudge is a lasting feeling of anger or dislike; hold a grudge against someone means remain resentful towards them
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She still held a grudge against her former colleague.” (Cô vẫn oán giận người đồng nghiệp cũ.)
