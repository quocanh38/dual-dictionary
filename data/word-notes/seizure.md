### Điểm cần nhớ
- have a seizure; seizure of assets; seizure disorder
### Dễ nhầm
- seizure can mean a sudden attack of illness or the act of taking property by authority; a seizure may require medical attention
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The court ordered the seizure of the illegal goods.” (Tòa ra lệnh tịch thu hàng hóa bất hợp pháp.)
