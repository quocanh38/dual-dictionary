### Điểm cần nhớ
- civilian population/life/clothes; civilian worker
### Dễ nhầm
- civilian is a person who is not a member of the armed forces or police; civil is broader and can mean relating to citizens/law
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The evacuation plan gives priority to civilians.” (Kế hoạch sơ tán ưu tiên dân thường.)
