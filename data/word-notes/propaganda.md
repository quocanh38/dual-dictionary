### Điểm cần nhớ
- political propaganda; propaganda campaign; spread propaganda
### Dễ nhầm
- propaganda is information designed to influence opinions, often by presenting one side selectively; it usually has a negative connotation
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The film was criticized as propaganda.” (Bộ phim bị chỉ trích là tuyên truyền một chiều.)
