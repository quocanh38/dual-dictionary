### Điểm cần nhớ
- presumably because; presumably the reason; can express a cautious assumption
### Dễ nhầm
- presumably signals what the speaker thinks is likely, not confirmed fact; presumably is not the same as definitely
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Presumably, the shop is closed because the lights are off.” (Có lẽ cửa hàng đóng cửa vì đèn đã tắt.)
