### Điểm cần nhớ
- Psst, come here; make a psst sound; psst for attention
### Dễ nhầm
- psst is an informal sound used to attract someone's attention quietly; it is an interjection rather than a regular word
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- ““Psst, the meeting is starting,” she whispered.” (“Suỵt, cuộc họp bắt đầu rồi,” cô thì thầm.)
