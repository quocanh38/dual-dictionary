### Điểm cần nhớ
- deceive someone into doing; deceive yourself; deceive → make someone believe falsely
### Dễ nhầm
- to deceive someone is to make them believe something untrue, often deliberately; deceive yourself means refuse to accept the truth
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The advertisement deceived customers about the price.” (Quảng cáo đã đánh lừa khách hàng về giá cả.)
