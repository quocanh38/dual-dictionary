### Điểm cần nhớ
- devoted to family; devoted supporter; devote → devoted
### Dễ nhầm
- devoted means giving someone or something a great deal of love, loyalty or effort; use devoted to before a noun or -ing form
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She is devoted to helping young musicians.” (Cô tận tâm giúp đỡ các nhạc sĩ trẻ.)
