### Điểm cần nhớ
- crushing defeat; crushing pressure; crushing → extremely severe
### Dễ nhầm
- crushing usually intensifies a bad or difficult experience, as in a crushing defeat; it can also literally describe something that crushes
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The team suffered a crushing defeat in the final.” (Đội bóng chịu thất bại nặng nề trong trận chung kết.)
