### Điểm cần nhớ
- western saloon; saloon bar; saloon car
### Dễ nhầm
- a saloon is a bar, especially in stories about the American West; in British English it can also mean a sedan car
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The old saloon stood at the end of the street.” (Quán rượu kiểu miền Tây cũ nằm cuối phố.)
