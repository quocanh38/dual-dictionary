### Điểm cần nhớ
- spitting rain; spitting distance; spitting at someone
### Dễ nhầm
- spitting is the -ing form of spit; spitting rain describes very light rain, especially in informal British English
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “It was spitting when we left the house.” (Trời lất phất mưa khi chúng tôi rời nhà.)
