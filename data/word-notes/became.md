### Điểm cần nhớ
- became interested/known; became a teacher; become → became → become
### Dễ nhầm
- became is the simple past of become; the past participle is become, not became
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The quiet street became busy after the market opened.” (Con phố yên tĩnh trở nên nhộn nhịp sau khi chợ mở cửa.)
