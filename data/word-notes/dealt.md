### Điểm cần nhớ
- deal with a problem; dealt a card; deal → dealt
### Dễ nhầm
- dealt is the past tense and past participle of deal; deal with means handle, while deal someone a card means give them a card
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She dealt with the complaint calmly.” (Cô ấy xử lý lời phàn nàn một cách bình tĩnh.)
