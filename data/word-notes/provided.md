### Điểm cần nhớ
- provided that; provided somebody with something; provide → provided
### Dễ nhầm
- provided can mean supplied or, in “provided that,” on the condition that; supplied emphasizes giving something needed
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The hotel provided towels for every guest.” (Khách sạn cung cấp khăn cho mỗi vị khách.)
