### Điểm cần nhớ
- becoming a parent; becoming clear; a becoming dress (formal)
### Dễ nhầm
- becoming is the -ing form of become; as an adjective it can mean flattering/appropriate, a less common use
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The reason for the delay is becoming clear.” (Lý do chậm trễ đang dần trở nên rõ ràng.)
