### Điểm cần nhớ
- delayed flight; delayed response; delay → late or held back
### Dễ nhầm
- delayed is the past form of delay and can describe something that happens later than planned; a delayed flight is held back, not cancelled
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The delayed train finally arrived at noon.” (Chuyến tàu bị hoãn cuối cùng cũng đến vào buổi trưa.)
