### Điểm cần nhớ
- water fountain; fountain pen; fountain → source sending water upward
### Dễ nhầm
- a fountain is a structure that sends water into the air; a fountain pen is a refillable pen that uses liquid ink
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Children played beside the fountain.” (Bọn trẻ chơi bên đài phun nước.)
