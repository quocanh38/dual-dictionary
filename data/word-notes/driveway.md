### Điểm cần nhớ
- park in the driveway; driveway entrance; driveway → private access road
### Dễ nhầm
- a driveway is a private hard path or road leading from a street to a house or garage; it is not the public road itself
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She parked the car in the driveway.” (Cô đỗ xe trên lối vào nhà.)
