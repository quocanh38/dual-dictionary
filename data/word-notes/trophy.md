### Điểm cần nhớ
- win a trophy; trophy cabinet; trophy wife
### Dễ nhầm
- a trophy is a prize given for success; trophy wife is a dated, often disapproving expression for a spouse seen as a status symbol
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The team lifted the trophy after the final.” (Đội nâng chiếc cúp sau trận chung kết.)
