### Điểm cần nhớ
- rabbit warren; Warren (given name/surname); warren → burrow network
### Dễ nhầm
- a warren is a network of underground rabbit tunnels or a crowded, confusing place; it is also a personal name
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The field was dotted with rabbit warrens.” (Cánh đồng có nhiều hang thỏ.)
