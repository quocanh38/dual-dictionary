### Điểm cần nhớ
- faculty of medicine; teaching faculty; faculty → department or ability
### Dễ nhầm
- faculty can mean a university department or its teaching staff; it can also mean a natural mental or physical ability
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She joined the faculty of engineering.” (Cô gia nhập giảng viên khoa kỹ thuật.)
