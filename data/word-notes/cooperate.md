### Điểm cần nhớ
- cooperate with somebody; cooperate on a project; cooperate fully
### Dễ nhầm
- cooperate means work together willingly; collaborate often suggests working jointly on a specific task
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The two departments agreed to cooperate on the research.” (Hai phòng ban đồng ý hợp tác trong nghiên cứu.)
