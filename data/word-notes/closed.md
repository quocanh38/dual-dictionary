### Điểm cần nhớ
- be closed; closed on Sundays; close → closed
### Dễ nhầm
- closed describes a state or is the past form; close (adjective) means near and is pronounced differently
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The museum is closed for maintenance this week.” (Bảo tàng đóng cửa để bảo trì trong tuần này.)
