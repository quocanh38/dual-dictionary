### Điểm cần nhớ
- fleet of cars; naval fleet; fleet-footed
### Dễ nhầm
- a fleet is a group of ships, vehicles or aircraft under one owner or command; navy is a country’s sea force
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The airline added ten planes to its fleet.” (Hãng hàng không bổ sung mười máy bay vào đội bay.)
