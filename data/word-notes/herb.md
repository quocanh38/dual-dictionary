### Điểm cần nhớ
- fresh herbs; herb garden; herb → plant used for flavour or medicine
### Dễ nhầm
- an herb is a plant whose leaves, flowers or seeds are used in cooking or medicine; pronunciation of the initial h varies by dialect
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Add fresh herbs just before serving.” (Thêm rau thơm ngay trước khi dọn món.)
