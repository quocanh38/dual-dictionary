### Điểm cần nhớ
- bless somebody; bless you after a sneeze; bless somebody with something
### Dễ nhầm
- bless has religious uses and informal expressions; blessed is the common past form and adjective
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Her neighbors blessed her with a basket of garden vegetables.” (Hàng xóm tặng cô một giỏ rau vườn đầy ắp.)
