### Điểm cần nhớ
- courageous decision; courageous enough to; courageous → brave
### Dễ nhầm
- courageous means showing courage, especially when facing danger or difficulty; it is more formal than brave
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “It was courageous of her to speak out.” (Cô ấy thật dũng cảm khi lên tiếng.)
