### Điểm cần nhớ
- dye your hair; fabric dye; dye → colour with a substance
### Dễ nhầm
- to dye is to change the colour of something using a colouring substance; dye is pronounced like die but has a different meaning
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She dyed the fabric a deep blue.” (Cô nhuộm tấm vải thành màu xanh đậm.)
