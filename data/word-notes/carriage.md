### Điểm cần nhớ
- horse-drawn carriage; carriage return; carriage → rail car (UK)
### Dễ nhầm
- a carriage is a vehicle pulled by horses or a passenger section of a train; carriage can also mean carrying something
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “They rode through the park in a horse-drawn carriage.” (Họ đi xe ngựa qua công viên.)
