### Điểm cần nhớ
- sign a petition; petition the court; petition for change
### Dễ nhầm
- a petition is a formal written request signed by people; as a verb it means formally ask an authority
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Residents signed a petition to save the park.” (Cư dân ký đơn kiến nghị cứu công viên.)
