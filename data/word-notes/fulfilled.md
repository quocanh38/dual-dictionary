### Điểm cần nhớ
- feel fulfilled; fulfilled a promise; fulfil → satisfied/completed
### Dễ nhầm
- fulfilled can mean happy and satisfied, or completed as promised; the British spelling of the verb is fulfil, while US English also uses fulfill
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “She felt fulfilled by her work with children.” (Cô cảm thấy mãn nguyện với công việc cùng trẻ em.)
