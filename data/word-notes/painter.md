### Điểm cần nhớ
- oil painter; house painter; painter and decorator
### Dễ nhầm
- a painter is an artist who paints pictures or a worker who paints buildings; the context distinguishes the jobs
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The painter finished the portrait in three weeks.” (Họa sĩ hoàn thành bức chân dung trong ba tuần.)
