### Điểm cần nhớ
- organ transplant; transplant a tree; transplant surgery
### Dễ nhầm
- a transplant is an organ or tissue moved to another body; as a verb it means move a living thing or tissue to a new place
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “Doctors performed a kidney transplant.” (Các bác sĩ đã tiến hành ghép thận.)
