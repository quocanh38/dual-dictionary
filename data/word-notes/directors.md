### Điểm cần nhớ
- board of directors; film directors; director → person who manages or directs
### Dễ nhầm
- directors is the plural of director, a person who manages an organisation or guides a film or performance; one person is a director
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The directors approved the new budget.” (Các giám đốc đã thông qua ngân sách mới.)
