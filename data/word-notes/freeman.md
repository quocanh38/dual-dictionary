### Điểm cần nhớ
- freeman of a city; medieval freeman; freeman → person with certain legal rights
### Dễ nhầm
- freeman is a historical or legal term for a man with full civic or personal rights; it is not simply a synonym for any free person
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “The town made the artist an honorary freeman.” (Thị trấn trao danh hiệu công dân danh dự cho nghệ sĩ.)
