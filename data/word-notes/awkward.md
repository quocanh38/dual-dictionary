### Điểm cần nhớ
- an awkward silence/question; feel awkward; awkwardly
### Dễ nhầm
- awkward describes something uncomfortable, difficult to handle or poorly arranged; clumsy focuses more on movement/skill
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- “There was an awkward pause after the unexpected question.” (Có khoảng lặng ngượng ngùng sau câu hỏi bất ngờ.)
