### Điểm cần nhớ
- whoo! as an informal sound of excitement or surprise; whoo-hoo is a more established spelling
### Dễ nhầm
- whoo is an expressive spelling with no stable dictionary meaning in the two checked editions; use woo-hoo for clear excitement or whoa to stop
### Mẫu câu giá trị
- ““Whoo, we made it before the doors closed!”” (“Chà, chúng ta đến kịp trước khi cửa đóng rồi!”)
