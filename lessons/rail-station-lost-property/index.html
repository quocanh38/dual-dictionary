Lost Property at Central Station

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use two phrases for reporting and claiming lost items in service settings.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

Passengers who find a wallet or bag at Central Station should hand it in to the information desk rather than leave it on a seat. Staff attach a numbered tag and record the time and location where the item was found. A person collecting it may need to give proof of ownership, such as a clear description of an item inside, before staff release it. The desk stores small objects in a locked cabinet and keeps larger bags in a supervised room. Items are recorded in a daily register so the next shift can answer questions. If a passenger thinks they have lost something, they should describe it without sharing sensitive account numbers in public. The station will contact the owner if a safe contact detail is available.
