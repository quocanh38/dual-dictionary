Station Notices: Maintenance and Service Updates

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand the meanings of carry out maintenance and out of service in a station notice and use them in natural workplace messages.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The station manager will carry out maintenance on the north entrance escalator next Tuesday. While the work is underway, the escalator will be out of service and signs will direct passengers to the lifts. Staff will inspect the warning boards before the morning rush and report any blocked route to the duty manager. The notice asks passengers to allow a few extra minutes when changing platforms.

Vocabulary in context

carry out maintenance

to perform work to repair or keep equipment in good condition

thực hiện bảo trì
