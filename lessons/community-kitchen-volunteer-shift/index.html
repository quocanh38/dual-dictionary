Sharing Closing Duties in the Community Kitchen

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use 'take turns' and 'is responsible for' to describe shared workplace duties.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

Volunteers at River Street community kitchen share a simple closing routine. Two people take turns checking the refrigerator temperatures and noting the results on a daily sheet. Each volunteer is responsible for one part of the room: the preparation table, serving counter, or dish area. Before leaving, put sealed ingredients back in labelled storage and wipe work surfaces with the approved cleaner. If a label is missing, do not guess what is inside; place the container in the marked review tray and ask the shift lead. The lead checks the closing sheet at 7 p.m. and leaves a short message about anything that needs attention before the next team arrives. New volunteers can ask for a demonstration during handover.
