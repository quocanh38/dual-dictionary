Library Survey: Choosing to Participate and Sharing Information

Academic English · B2 (estimated)

By the end of the lesson, learners will understand the meanings of opt out and contact details and use them accurately in everyday messages.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

A school committee is asking students how they use the quiet study area in the library. The short survey does not request names, student numbers or contact details. Participation is optional, and students may opt out at any time without giving a reason. Two committee coordinators can read the original responses, which will be kept confidential while the results are reviewed. The final report will combine comments into broad themes and will not quote a response that could identify a student. Teachers will receive the summary after the committee checks it for private details. Students can ask the librarian how the information will be used before they decide whether to take part.
