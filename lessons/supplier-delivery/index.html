Checking Deliveries and Contacting Suppliers

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use two workplace phrases for comparing goods with an order and for contacting someone.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The warehouse team will check the delivery against the order before signing the receipt. If an item is missing, a supervisor will get in touch with the supplier that afternoon. Staff will photograph the unopened boxes and keep the order number beside the checklist. This record helps both teams explain which items arrived without blaming a colleague who was not present.
