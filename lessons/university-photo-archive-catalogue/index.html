Cataloguing Archive Photographs Carefully

Academic English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use work in pairs and in chronological order to describe academic tasks accurately.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

A university archive is inviting students to help catalogue photographs donated by local businesses. Students work in pairs and enter only details visible in each image, such as a shop sign or a date printed on the back. If a photograph shows a familiar street but no exact address, the catalogue entry records the uncertainty rather than guessing. Visitors can use a searchable index to find a topic and then open the original scan for context. Records with known dates appear in chronological order, while uncertain dates are kept in a separate group. When students mention a photograph in their notes, they refer to its catalogue number so another reader can locate the same item. Staff will check a sample of entries before the collection becomes available online.
