Refill Shop Supplies

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learn to use 'run low' and 'top up' to talk about supplies at work.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

Volunteers at the East Dock Refill Shop check the shelves before the doors open. They compare each jar with the stock card and write down items that are nearly empty. If soap or cooking oil starts to run low, the shift lead contacts the supplier before the weekend. At closing time, volunteers top up the display bottles from sealed containers in the storeroom. They label each bottle with the product name and refill date. The team keeps cleaning liquids on a separate shelf from food products. A weekly count helps the shop decide which items to order for the next delivery.
