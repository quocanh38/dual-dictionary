Planning Academic Travel

Academic English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will understand and correctly use make arrangements for and provide evidence of in academic contexts.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The department will make arrangements for students to attend a regional conference. Each applicant must provide evidence of how the event relates to their research. Because the travel fund is limited, the panel will compare the purpose and expected benefit of each visit. Students should describe a specific session they plan to attend rather than simply listing the speakers they admire.
