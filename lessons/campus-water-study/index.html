Collecting Information and Making Careful Judgments

Academic English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use 'gather evidence' and 'take into consideration' to describe careful academic work.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

A student research group will gather evidence about water use in two campus buildings. Before drawing a conclusion, the group must take into consideration the different numbers of people using each building. They will compare measurements from ordinary teaching weeks rather than holidays. Their report will explain the limits of the sample instead of claiming that one small study proves which building is always more efficient.
