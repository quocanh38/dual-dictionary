Making Lectures Accessible

Academic English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use take steps and identify barriers to discuss improving access to academic events.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The museum will take steps to make its public lectures easier to attend. Organisers need to identify barriers that prevent visitors from joining the discussion. They will ask participants about seating, sound and the timing of events before changing the programme. The report will separate observations from suggestions so that the committee can decide which changes to test first.
