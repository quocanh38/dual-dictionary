Handing Over Work and Tracking Requests

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand hand over and keep track of from a workplace handover and use them in new contexts.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

Before the project lead leaves for annual leave, she will hand over the client schedule to her colleague. They will go over the open tasks in a short video call and flag any deadline that may change. The colleague will keep track of new requests in the shared log and follow up with the design team if a file is missing. A clear handover helps everyone know who is responsible while the lead is away.
