Planning Ahead at Work and at the Clinic

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use in advance and routine appointment to manage time and bookings politely.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

At the Riverside clinic, the front desk has changed its appointment process for the winter. Patients who need another time should call reception at least one day in advance so staff can offer the unused slot to someone else. Anyone arriving with a new prescription should bring the medicine list from their last visit. If a patient cannot attend, the clinic asks them to cancel rather than leave a space empty. On busy mornings, the waiting-room display shows estimated delays; staff update it every thirty minutes. Patients with urgent symptoms should speak directly to reception instead of waiting for a routine appointment.
