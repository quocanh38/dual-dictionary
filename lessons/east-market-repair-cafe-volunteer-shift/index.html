Repair Café Work: Shifts and Supplies

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and correctly use sign up for a shift and runs out of in workplace contexts.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

At the East Market repair café, volunteers meet at 9 a.m. to prepare shared tools for a free repair session. New volunteers sign up for a shift using the schedule beside the entrance. The shift lead checks each tool and places items that need attention in a labelled tray. If the café runs out of a common part, volunteers record the item and tell the visitor what can be repaired that day. They do not promise a completion time until a technician has inspected the item. At closing, the lead passes on any unfinished requests to the next team. Visitors may wait in the seating area while a repair is in progress.
