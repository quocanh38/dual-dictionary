Workplace Scheduling: Bring Forward and Put Off

Workplace English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use bring forward and put off to describe moving work events earlier or later.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

After reviewing the volunteer rota, the project coordinator decided to bring forward the equipment check by one day because the meeting room is booked on Friday. The change does not affect the public event, which remains on Sunday. If delivery of the display boards is delayed, the team may put off the optional walkthrough until more volunteers are available. The coordinator will confirm any change by email, so volunteers should not rely on an old printed schedule. Anyone unable to attend can suggest another time, but the shared calendar will be updated only after the venue approves it. Volunteers are asked to reply by Wednesday so the coordinator can arrange enough people for each task.
