Registering for a Class: Forms and Waiting Lists

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and correctly use 'fill in the online form' and 'waiting list' in everyday service and workplace situations.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The Riverside Centre opens registration for its spring pottery classes on Monday. Each class meets once a week for six weeks, and the fee includes clay and access to shared tools. New participants should fill in the online form and choose a preferred class time. A confirmation email will arrive within two working days if a place is available. When a class is full, the centre puts applicants on a waiting list in the order their forms were received. Staff contact people on that list if a place opens before the first session. Participants who need step-free access can contact the front desk before registering so staff can confirm which room is suitable.
