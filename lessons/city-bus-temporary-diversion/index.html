Temporary Routes and Building Access

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and correctly use remain in effect and step-free access in workplace notices.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The city transport team will close Market Street to buses from 8 p.m. Friday while crews repair a damaged drainage cover. Route 6 will use Oak Avenue in both directions; the diversion will remain in effect until inspectors confirm the road is safe. Digital signs at three stops will show the temporary route, and drivers will announce the change before leaving the central station. The team will review passenger reports on Monday and adjust the notice if any stop information is unclear. Passengers who need step-free access should check the updated stop map, because two temporary stops have no raised platform.
