Testing Flexible Start Times at Work

Workplace English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will understand and correctly use phase in and take effect in workplace contexts.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

A regional design firm will test flexible start times at its two largest offices. Managers plan to phase in the change over six weeks so staff can report problems before it applies to every team. The new schedule will take effect on 1 December, after the trial and a review with employee representatives. Staff may begin between 7:30 and 9:30, but each team must keep its client-support hours covered. Supervisors will compare attendance patterns and service records with the previous month. If the arrangement creates gaps at reception, the firm will adjust the team rota before extending the policy to other offices.
