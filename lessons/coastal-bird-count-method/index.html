Comparing Counts and Describing Trends

Academic English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will understand and correctly use 'account for' and 'long-term decline' to describe data and trends in academic contexts.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

A university ecology group is comparing monthly bird counts from two coastal wetlands. Because volunteers used different observation times in the first year, the analyst will account for the length of each visit before comparing totals. The team will also check the new counts against the field log to identify missing entries. Results will be reported separately for each wetland, since the sites differ in vegetation and visitor access. The researchers plan to share the method with local conservation officers so they can repeat the survey next spring. Any estimate that relies on a small number of observations will be described as provisional, not as evidence of a long-term decline.
