Podcast Notes: Off the Record and Time Stamp

Academic English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand the meanings of off the record and time stamp in the podcast context and use them naturally in new situations.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

A student media group is preparing a short podcast about how older buildings are reused. After recording three interviews with local planners, the students listen to the files together. They pick out brief sections that explain a design choice clearly, then note the time stamp so the editor can find each part. If an interviewee asks for a comment to stay off the record, the group leaves that section out of the public episode. The editor checks each excerpt against the full conversation to make sure its meaning has not changed. Before release, the team sends the final audio to the speakers for a factual check.
