Tree Survey Findings

Academic English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will understand carry out a survey as conducting a systematic study and use point out to introduce an observation.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

A university team will carry out a survey of trees along three busy streets. Researchers will record the species, trunk width and distance from the road at each site. In the preliminary report, they point out that young trees are concentrated near newer apartment blocks. The team will compare these observations with summer temperature readings before recommending where the city should plant more trees.
