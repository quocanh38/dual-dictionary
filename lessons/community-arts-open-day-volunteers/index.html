Helping at the Hillview Open Day

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use 'take part in' and 'in charge of' to describe participation and responsibility at work.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The Hillview Arts Centre will hold an open day next month so residents can see its new workshop rooms. Volunteers may take part in a short welcome session before visitors arrive. One coordinator is in charge of the welcome desk, while other volunteers guide people to the printmaking and pottery rooms. The centre will provide signs, name badges and a simple room map. Volunteers who cannot attend the session can read the same instructions in the shared folder. The event is free, but visitors need to register at the entrance because each workshop has limited space. After the open day, the team will meet briefly to note which signs were easy to follow and which rooms need clearer directions.
