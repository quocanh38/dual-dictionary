Fair Comparisons and Provisional Estimates in Field Research

Academic English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will interpret fair comparison and provisional estimate in context and use them accurately in new academic situations.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

A university geography group is comparing afternoon temperatures along two city walking routes. The researchers record shade coverage, the percentage of each route shaded by trees or buildings, beside every surface-temperature reading. To make a fair comparison, observers visit both routes at the same time on several clear days. The first set of measurements is a provisional estimate because one sensor stopped working during the second visit. The team will check the missing interval against field notes before calculating a weekly average. A single hot afternoon cannot show that one route is usually warmer. The final report will describe the method, identify limitations and include the route map so another group can repeat the survey.
