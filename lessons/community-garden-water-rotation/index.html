Learners will understand and correctly use allocate water to and in line with in workplace messages.

Reading

Before the dry season, the community garden team will allocate water to each plot according to the crops and the weekly forecast. Volunteers record the volume used so they can adjust the schedule in line with changes in rainfall. The coordinator asks each grower to report leaks as soon as they are found. This helps the team conserve water without leaving young plants dry.
