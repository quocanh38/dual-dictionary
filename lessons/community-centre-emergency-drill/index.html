Workplace Safety Practice

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use fire drill and assembly point in workplace safety contexts.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

Northside community centre will hold a fire drill on Thursday at 10:30 a.m. Staff should keep the east exit clear and guide visitors to the assembly point beside the library. In case of a real alarm, leave bags behind and do not use the lifts. The floor warden will sign off the checklist after confirming that each room is empty. Anyone who needs step-free assistance should tell the front desk before the drill so the team can arrange a partner and use the north ramp. The exercise is only practice; normal classes will resume after a short briefing. Staff should report blocked routes to the centre manager before the drill begins.
