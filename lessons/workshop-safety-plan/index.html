Workplace Safety: Inspections and Rules

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use carry out an inspection and comply with the safety rules in workplace contexts.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The workshop supervisor will carry out an inspection before a new machine is used. The team must comply with the safety rules, even when several orders are waiting. A short training session will show staff where to stop the machine and whom to contact if something goes wrong. The supervisor will ask a colleague to check that the written instructions match the controls on the machine.
