Workplace Handover: hand over and follow up on

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand and use hand over and follow up on to describe workplace responsibility and unfinished tasks.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The evening receptionist will hand over the booking records to the night team before leaving. Staff must follow up on guest requests that were not resolved during the day. A guest who needs an early taxi should receive a clear confirmation before the morning shift begins. The shared checklist shows who is responsible for each request, so guests do not need to explain the same problem again.
