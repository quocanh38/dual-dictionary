Reserving Time and Taking Care at the Food Bank

Workplace English · B1 (estimated)

Learners will understand set aside and look after in context and use them naturally in new everyday situations.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

The coordinator will set aside time for a short briefing before the food-bank opens on Saturday. New volunteers will learn where to store donated goods and how to look after chilled items. During the first hour, a team leader will hand out labels and assign each person a shelf to check. At the end of the shift, volunteers will record low-stock items so the next team can prepare an order.
