Lab Audits: Using carry out and account for

Academic English · B2 (estimated)

Learners will interpret carry out and account for in an academic lab context and use them accurately in new situations.

Open exercises in HọcTA

Reading

A research assistant will carry out a quarterly audit of shared laboratory equipment. The team will account for each device by matching its label with the inventory record. In response to several missing calibration dates, the supervisor has added a reminder field to the maintenance log. The next review will check whether staff have entered the dates consistently and whether any device needs servicing before the study begins.
